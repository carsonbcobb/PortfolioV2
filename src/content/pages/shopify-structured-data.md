ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode decide what to recommend from what they can read about your products. On most Shopify stores that data is incomplete, duplicated or contradicts itself. A review app adds a second Product block. Variants are missing prices or identifiers. The facts a shopper asks about, like materials, sizing, ingredients or who it's for, aren't in the data at all. I fix that directly in your theme, so every product tells AI the same clear story.

I'm Carson Cobb, an independent [AI search](/ai-search) and CRO specialist for Shopify brands. I do the work myself, for Shopify brands in every product category.

## What I fix

- **Duplicate or conflicting Product schema** from your theme and apps, merged into one clean block
- **Product, Offer and variant data:** prices, availability, GTINs and SKUs on every variant
- **Review and rating data** that matches what's on the page
- **Product attributes AI uses to match products to questions,** built from metafields so your team can edit them in Shopify
- **Organization, Brand, Breadcrumb, FAQ, shipping and return policy markup**
- **Validation,** so Google reads it cleanly and nothing breaks on the next app update

## How it works

I audit your current structured data across your catalog and show you exactly what AI sees today. Then I write the JSON-LD by hand into your theme, connect it to metafields where your team needs to edit it, test it, and ship it with your sign off. No schema app layered on top.

That's how it works on both [Close the Gap](/close-the-gap) plans, where I fix what AI gets wrong about your products on your site myself. Structured data is one part of the work: it sits alongside product pages that answer the questions shoppers ask AI, the outside sources AI relies on, and a report that shows whether AI's answers change. Every price is on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Why it matters

In my research, ChatGPT gave the right price for [all 20 tested products that had product structured data](/reports/does-schema-change-what-chatgpt-says), and couldn't find a price for 2 of the 4 that had none. [29% of 94 Shopify stores published duplicate product data](/reports/duplicate-product-schema-ai-visibility), usually from a review app.

Ratings need the same care. In my [star ratings research](/reports/star-ratings-reviews-ai-answers), 79 of 82 rated products on Google AI Mode's cards were 4.0 stars or higher, so rating data that's missing or wrong can keep a product off the list.
