**By Carson Cobb, Fractional Head of Ecommerce. First published October 31, 2026. Updated November 1, 2026 with a larger check.**

I checked 606 Shopify stores across six categories, 121,519 products in total, for the same things I look at first in every store audit: whether reorderable products offer subscribe and save, whether best sellers are in stock, whether gifting is set up before the holidays, and whether bundles exist.

This page is the overview. Each topic has its own in depth report in [Reports](/reports).

> **Update, November 1:** the first version checked 8 products per store and looked for gift collections at a few common addresses. This version checks up to 20 products per store and reads every published collection, so the numbers below are more complete. The biggest change is gifting: far more stores have gift collections than the first check could see.

## The short version

- **28% of brands selling reorderable products offer no subscription at all.** Across 434 supplement, food, beauty and pet stores, 72% had a selling plan on at least one checked product.
- **Very few cover everything.** Only 13% of reorderable brands offered subscribe and save on every product checked.
- **Best sellers are where it hurts most.** Of reorderable brands with a best sellers collection, only 39% offered subscribe and save on every best seller checked. 20% offered it on none of them.
- **A quarter of best sellers collections have something sold out.** 24% of stores with a best sellers collection had at least one product in it fully out of stock.
- **A quarter of stores have no gifting setup eight weeks before Christmas.** 25% had neither a gift collection nor a gift card. In supplements it was 45%.

## Subscriptions by category

Share of stores offering subscribe and save on the products checked (best sellers first, up to 20 per store).

| Category | Stores | Any product | Every product | None |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supplements | 120 | 87% | 25% | 13% |
| Pet | 41 | 68% | 12% | 32% |
| Food and beverage | 149 | 67% | 9% | 33% |
| Beauty and skincare | 124 | 66% | 7% | 34% |
| Home | 77 | 32% | 1% | 68% |
| Apparel | 95 | 3% | 1% | 97% |

Supplements lead because the model is proven there. Food, beauty and pet are where the biggest gap sits: these are products people run out of and buy again, and a third of those stores still make every reorder a fresh purchase.

Not every gap is a mistake. Some products are seasonal, limited or sold as one time gifts, and some brands choose not to discount repeat orders. But when the hero product someone reorders every month has no subscribe option, those reorders can easily drift to a competitor or to Amazon.

The discounts, frequencies and apps behind these offers are in the [subscription benchmarks report](/reports/shopify-subscription-benchmarks-2026).

## Partial subscriptions are the most common setup

Among reorderable brands that offer subscriptions, the typical store covered 70% of the products checked. Often the best seller has it and the newer products do not, or the reverse. In the stores I audit, it tends to come from one of two things:

1. Selling plans were added product by product when the subscription app was installed, and new products were never added.
2. Bundles and variants were created later without a selling plan attached.

Both are quick fixes once someone looks.

## Sold out best sellers

| Category | Stores with a best sellers collection | Of those, at least one sold out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | 77% | 31% |
| Food and beverage | 56% | 30% |
| Beauty and skincare | 81% | 28% |
| Pet | 71% | 21% |
| Apparel | 89% | 19% |
| Supplements | 72% | 16% |

A sold out product in the best sellers collection is the most visible stockout a store can have, since that collection is often linked from the menu or homepage. Beyond best sellers, 34% of all stores had 10% or more of their catalog fully sold out. Food had the highest typical share, which fits seasonal and small batch runs. More in the [catalog and stock report](/reports/shopify-catalog-and-stock-report-2026).

## Gifting before the holidays

| Category | Gift collection with products | Gift card product | Neither |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apparel | 91% | 69% | 3% |
| Beauty and skincare | 77% | 60% | 19% |
| Home | 71% | 57% | 10% |
| Food and beverage | 55% | 54% | 31% |
| Pet | 54% | 41% | 39% |
| Supplements | 29% | 39% | 45% |

Supplements and pet are the least ready for gift shoppers. A gift card alone covers the last week before the holidays, when shipping cutoffs have passed. More in the [holiday readiness report](/reports/holiday-readiness-report-2026).

## Bundles and pricing

81% of stores had a bundle, kit, set or pack collection, so bundles are standard now. The question is whether they are easy to find and whether they carry a subscription. The typical product price (the median of each store's median, leaving out $0 items) was $35 overall, $25 in food, $32 in beauty, $39 in supplements and $80 in apparel. That number matters for where you set your free shipping line; the [free shipping calculator](/tools/free-shipping-calculator) works it out from your own order value.

## What to check on your own store this week

1. Open your top 5 sellers and confirm each one offers subscribe and save if people reorder it.
2. Check that every bundle and every new product launched this year has the same selling plan as your hero.
3. Look at your best sellers collection on mobile. Anything sold out should either come off the collection or show a back in stock signup.
4. Make sure a gift card and a gift collection are live and linked before mid November.
5. Run the numbers on what a better subscription offer is worth with the [subscription calculator](/tools/subscription-calculator).

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 606 US Shopify stores priced in US dollars in supplements (120), beauty and skincare (124), food and beverage (149), pet (41), home (77) and apparel (95). Mostly small and mid size direct to consumer brands. Stores with fewer than 3 products were excluded.
- **Data:** each store's public product data (up to 1,000 products) and every published collection, checked November 1, 2026. Gift card products were excluded from stock and price counts.
- **Subscriptions:** up to 20 products per store were checked for active selling plans, taken from the best sellers collection first, then the catalog. Pre order and try before you buy plans were excluded. A store offering subscriptions only through a separate page or a quiz would show as none.
- **Best sellers:** the store's own collection with best seller, top seller, most popular or fan favorite in its address or title.
- **Gifting:** published collections with gift, holiday, Christmas, Xmas or stocking in the address or title and at least one product, plus gift card products.
- **Bundles:** published collections with products whose address contains bundle, kit, set, pack, sampler, stack or build your own.
