## What is AI search?

Shoppers no longer type three words into a search bar and click through ten results. They describe their exact situation to an AI assistant: "I have sensitive skin and fragrance makes it worse, what's a good natural moisturizer?" The assistant answers with a short list of products, usually three to five, with a reason for each and links to buy.

That short list is the new shelf. If your product is on it, you get a shopper who has already compared options and been told why you fit. If it's not, a competing product gets that shopper, and you never see the lost sale in your analytics.

AI shoppers convert far better than other traffic because the research is already done. By the time they reach your store, they arrive ready to buy. That's why AI search and [conversion work](/shopify-conversion-optimization) belong together: one gets your products recommended, the other makes sure the store closes the sale.

## What do I do on your site?

* **Product data built for each product.** Complete, consistent facts about every product that matters, so AI can say what it is, who it's for and how it compares without guessing.
* **Product pages that answer buyer questions.** The facts shoppers ask AI about, written so AI can quote them accurately.
* **One clear story per product.** Themes and apps often describe the same product in conflicting ways. I make sure AI reads one consistent version.
* **Pages AI can reach.** Crawlers that can get to everything you want AI to see.

## What do I do off site?

* **Finding the sources AI relies on for your products.** Testing the questions your buyers ask shows which reviews, comparisons and articles AI leans on when it recommends a competing product.
* **Earning real coverage in them.** In depth reviews and real comparisons carry weight with AI. A page that just praises a product carries little.
* **Reviews that reflect your products**, so the platforms AI reads show what customers actually say.
* **Retail listings** that tell AI the same story your store does.

## How is AI search measured?

* **Recommendations for each product.** I test the questions your buyers ask about each product that matters, repeatedly, and track how often AI recommends you, where it places you, and whether what it says is accurate.
* **Signals from your store data and from customers.** Analytics tools undercount AI, so referral numbers are one input, not the whole picture.
