## The most common Shopify subscription leaks

These are real patterns I find in audits. Each one is small on its own. Together they can cut subscription revenue in half.

**Subscribe and save is missing on the hero product.** The brand set up subscriptions on a few products during launch, then added their daily product later and never attached a plan. The one item people buy most often has no way to subscribe.

**The selling plan exists but the widget never renders.** The plan is created in Shopify, the app shows it as active, but the theme was updated or swapped and the widget block was never added back. Customers literally cannot see the option.

**The default selection works against you.** One time purchase is preselected, the subscribe option sits below the fold, and the savings aren't shown in dollars. Customers take the path of least resistance.

**Trial and sample packs lead nowhere.** A brand sells a starter kit or sample pack, the customer loves it, and then there's no offer to roll into a subscription. The best moment to convert a buyer into a subscriber passes in silence.

**Cancellation is a single click.** No pause option, no skip, no swap to a different flavor or frequency, no offer to slow the shipping cadence. Many customers who cancel just have too much product. They'd stay if you asked.

**Subscribers get the same emails as everyone else.** No upcoming order reminder, no easy way to add an item to the next box, no failed payment recovery that sounds like a person. Subscribers are your best customers and they're being treated like strangers.

## What I actually do

I treat subscriptions as a system, not a setting. Here's the work, in the order I usually tackle it.

### Offer design

I start with the offer because nothing else matters if the offer is weak. That means the discount level your margins can carry, the frequencies that match how fast people actually use your product, whether to add a free gift on the first order or a loyalty step up at order three, and whether prepaid plans make sense. I base this on your real order data, not a template.

### Plan setup in Recharge or Skio

I build or clean up your selling plans so every product that should be subscribable is, with the right frequencies and the right discounts. I check that plans are attached to every variant, that bundles and stacks behave correctly, and that nothing breaks at checkout. I work in Recharge, Skio and native Shopify selling plans.

### Product page widget and default selection

This is where the biggest lifts usually come from. I make sure the widget renders on every relevant template, decide whether subscribe should be the default, show the savings clearly in dollars, and explain what happens next in one or two plain lines so customers aren't worried about getting stuck. Then I test it on real phones, because most of your traffic is mobile.

### Trial to subscription paths

If you sell a sample, starter kit or trial size, I connect it to a subscription offer. That can be a post purchase upsell, an email sequence timed to when the trial runs out, or a starter kit that converts into a subscription automatically. The goal is that no happy trial customer ever has to go looking for how to reorder.

### Cancellation flows

I set up a cancel flow that gives customers real alternatives before they leave: skip, pause, change frequency, swap the product, or take a one time offer. I also look at the reasons people give and feed them back into the offer and the product page.

### Klaviyo flows for subscribers

I build subscriber specific flows in Klaviyo: welcome, upcoming charge reminder, add to next order, failed payment recovery, win back after cancel, and milestone thank you emails. Each one is short and written to sound like your brand, not a billing system.
