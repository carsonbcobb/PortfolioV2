If you're on Recharge or Skio and it works, stay put and watch for the details both companies say are coming. If you were choosing Skio as an alternative to Recharge, that reason is gone, and Loop is now the main independent option.

I'm Carson Cobb, a subscription and CRO specialist. I set up and fix subscription programs on Recharge, Skio and Loop for Shopify brands, and I don't get paid by any of them. For all three apps side by side, see my main [Recharge vs Skio vs Loop comparison](/recharge-vs-skio-vs-loop).

## What was announced

Recharge's announcement says Skio is joining the Recharge team, and that together they power more than 20,000 brands and process over $20B in GMV a year. It says both platforms will keep operating as they have, and that more details on combined plans are coming in the months ahead.

Skio's announcement says the two are preserving both platforms, and continuing to ship new features on them, while they work out the best path forward. It says that over roughly the next 12 months, Skio will work with Recharge to combine the best of both. The Skio listing on the Shopify App Store is now titled Skio, a Recharge Company.

Neither announcement mentions pricing changes, a shutdown date for either platform, or a required migration.

## What it means for Recharge merchants

For now, nothing changes. Your plan, your fees and your team stay the same. Recharge Starter is $149/month plus 1.50% + 20¢ per transaction, and Scale is $499/month plus 1.25% + 20¢, as of November 2026. Over time, features from Skio may show up in Recharge, since both companies say they're reviewing both platforms.

## What it means for Skio merchants

Also nothing yet. Skio's Scale plan is $499/month billed annually or $599/month billed monthly, plus 1.25% + 20¢ per subscription order, and your Merchant Success Manager stays your contact. The open question is what combining the best of both looks like in 12 months. If you rely on a specific Skio feature, like the cancel flow builder, build a box or SkioSMS, ask your Skio contact how it fits into those plans.

## Features that matter for retention

The deal doesn't change what each app does today. Recharge has smart cancellation prevention and its customer portal on every plan, with bundles and Concierge SMS on Scale. Skio includes its multi step cancel flow builder, advanced build a box and SkioSMS on Scale. Loop includes personalized cancellation flows and bundles on every plan. Whatever happens with the merger, these are the features that keep subscribers, so they're the ones to protect in any move.

## Should you switch?

**If you're on Recharge or Skio and it works,** stay. A migration has real risk, and both companies say nothing is changing today.

**If you're choosing an app now,** pick on fit. My [main comparison](/recharge-vs-skio-vs-loop#which-one-id-pick-at-your-order-volume) has who I'd pick at each order volume.

**If you want to stay independent of Recharge,** Loop is the main option of the three. It includes white glove migration on every paid plan with no migration fee.

**If your subscription numbers are disappointing,** the app probably isn't the cause. Fix the offer, the product page widget and the cancel flow first. My [Shopify subscriptions page](/shopify-subscriptions) covers how.
