## The problems I see in beauty and skincare stores

### Product pages that don't answer the real questions

A skincare shopper wants to know three things fast. Will this work for my skin? How do I use it? What's in it?

Most product pages bury those answers under brand copy or hide them in tabs nobody opens. I rebuild the page so the first screen on mobile shows the product, the price, who it's for and the main result, with ingredients, directions and reviews right where doubt shows up.

Key ingredients, skin types and directions are built into your site, so every page stays consistent and accurate as your catalog changes.

### Shades and sizes that confuse people

Shade products are hard. If the swatch is wrong, the photo doesn't change, or the reviews don't show which shade someone bought, shoppers guess, and guessing leads to returns or no order at all.

I make sure the selected shade drives the photos, the stock status and, where your reviews app allows, the reviews shown. For sizes, I show what the larger size saves in plain dollars, because a bigger bottle is often the easiest order value win you have.

### No routine, just products

People don't buy skincare one product at a time. They build a routine. But most stores sell each product on its own and leave the customer to figure out what goes together.

I set up routines and bundles that make sense: cleanser, serum, moisturizer, or a starter set for a specific concern. With Rebuy or the theme, I show the right add on on the product page and in the cart, based on what customers actually buy together.

### Replenishment left to chance

A moisturizer runs out. A cleanser runs out. Most stores never ask for the reorder at the right time, so the customer replaces it with whatever is at the drugstore.

I set reorder timing based on how long each product actually lasts, then use it in two places: subscribe and save frequencies in Recharge or Skio, and replenishment emails in Klaviyo. More on the email side on my [Klaviyo email page](/klaviyo-email-for-shopify).

### Slow pages from heavy media

Beauty stores lean on video, big lifestyle photos, review widgets with photos, quizzes and popups. All of that adds weight, and the shopper coming from a creator's video won't wait.

I audit every app script and image, compress and resize media, and defer what doesn't need to load first. The goal is a product page that shows up before the shopper loses interest.

### Gift sets that show up too late

Holiday sets and gift bundles are big for beauty. They're also often built the week before the sale. I plan gift sets and promos on a calendar so the products, the pages, the cart offers and the emails are ready ahead of time.
