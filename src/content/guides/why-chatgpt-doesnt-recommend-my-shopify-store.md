**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 2026.**

You asked ChatGPT for the best product in your space, and it named four brands that weren't you. Maybe one of them is half your size. Here's what's actually going on, based on checking 94 Shopify stores and 72 real buyer questions across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode in November 2026.

## The short answer

ChatGPT recommends the brands it can find good reasons for. Most of those reasons come from other websites, not yours. Your own store decides whether the AI gets your facts right once it has chosen you. It rarely decides whether you get chosen.

## 1. Other sites are vouching for your competitors

In [48 buyer questions](/reports/sources-chatgpt-cites-2026), ChatGPT showed a source or a store link in 45 answers. Review and testing sites led, then magazines and publishers. In health related products, medical and research sources showed up in nearly a third of answers.

If the three articles ChatGPT trusts for "best magnesium for sleep" all name the same four brands, those four brands get named. That's the biggest single reason a good brand gets skipped.

**What to do:** search your own buyer questions, note which sources the AI cites, and work out how to get your product into those sources: reviews, comparison articles, expert roundups, Reddit threads that already rank.

## 2. You only win the narrow question

This is the pattern I see most often. A brand gets named when the question matches its exact product ("best roasted chickpea snack"), then disappears on the broader question shoppers actually ask ("healthy snacks to replace chips").

In my [wording test](/reports/how-question-wording-changes-ai-recommendations), rewording a question changed the top pick in most cases, and growing Shopify brands lost about a third of their mentions on longer, more specific questions. One question tells you almost nothing. You need the full set of questions a buyer asks on the way to your product.

## 3. Your product pages don't answer the questions

AI answers are built from facts: what it's made of, who it's for, how to use it, how it compares. Across 121,519 Shopify products, the typical description was [about 60 words](/reports/shopify-product-data-depth-2026). Only 23% of product pages explained how to use the product, and 16% compared it to anything.

If your page doesn't say your serum is fragrance free and made for sensitive skin, the AI can't recommend it for "fragrance free serum for sensitive skin."

## 4. The AI can't read your product data

Nobody I checked was blocking ChatGPT. But [11% of stores](/reports/ai-crawler-access-shopify) had product data that only appeared after scripts ran, and for some it was the only product data on the page. Review apps also added duplicate or detached rating blocks on [29% of stores](/reports/duplicate-product-schema-ai-visibility), which led to wrong ratings in AI answers.

## 5. Your ratings are below the bar, or wrong

In Google AI Mode, [79 of 82 rated product cards](/reports/star-ratings-reviews-ai-answers) were 4.0 stars or higher. Ratings work like a minimum bar: below 4.0 you rarely show, and above it other things decide the order. AI also misreads ratings often. All three AIs got the rating right for only 7 of 22 products I asked about directly.

## 6. You're checking the wrong AI

ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode [agree less than you'd think](/reports/chatgpt-gemini-ai-mode-brand-overlap-2026). Only 23% of the brands named came from all three. You can be invisible in ChatGPT and doing fine in AI Mode, or the reverse.

## What to fix first

1. **List 20 to 40 real buyer questions** for your top products, short and long, and run them in all three AIs.
2. **Record who gets named and which sources get cited.** That's your competition and your target list.
3. **Fix the product pages** for your best sellers so they answer the questions in plain sentences.
4. **Clean up product data** so price, rating and availability are readable without scripts and tied to the right product.
5. **Go after the sources** the AI already trusts for your questions.
