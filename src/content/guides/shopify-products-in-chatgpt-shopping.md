**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 2026.**

Two separate things have to happen for a shopper to buy from you through ChatGPT. Your products have to be **available** to ChatGPT, and ChatGPT has to **choose** them. Most guides only cover the first one. It's the easy part.

## Part 1: Make your products available

Shopify shares product data with ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog, managed in the **Agentic** sales channel. For most stores it's on by default.

**Check this in your admin:**

1. Go to **Sales channels > Agentic > ChatGPT** and confirm Shopify Catalog access is on.
2. In **Settings > Policies**, make sure your terms of service, privacy policy and return and refund policy are all filled in. Shopify requires them.
3. Make sure your store **sells to customers in the United States**. Your business can be based anywhere.
4. Check that the products you care about are **listed and not hidden from search engines**, and have a title, at least one image and a price above zero.

According to Shopify, checkout happens on your own store, in ChatGPT's in app browser or a new tab. So your product page and checkout still do the selling.

**Common reasons products are missing:** B2B only products aren't supported, unlisted or hidden products are excluded, and products restricted by OpenAI's commerce policies won't appear. Also check that your price and availability match between your catalog and the live page.

## Part 2: Get your products chosen

This is where most brands get stuck. Being available doesn't mean being recommended. ChatGPT names [about four brands per answer](/reports/who-chatgpt-recommends-2026), so you're competing for one of a handful of spots.

What decides it:

- **Outside sources.** ChatGPT showed a source in 45 of 48 product answers I tested, mostly review sites, testing sites and publishers. Brands those sources mention get named.
- **Product pages that answer questions.** The typical Shopify product description is about 60 words. The AI needs the facts the shopper asked about: ingredients or materials, who it's for, how to use it, how it compares.
- **Clean product data.** Price, rating and availability readable without scripts and attached to the right product. Duplicate rating blocks from review apps caused [many of the wrong ratings](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026) I saw.
- **Catalog fields.** In my sample, [30% of products had no barcode](/reports/shopify-catalog-data-ai-shopping-2026) on any variant and 21% had no product type. Barcodes help AI match your product across retailers and reviews.

## A simple order of work

1. Confirm eligibility in the Agentic channel and your policies (15 minutes).
2. Fill barcodes and product types on your best sellers.
3. Rewrite your top 10 product pages to answer the real buyer questions.
4. Fix duplicate or detached rating data from your review app.
5. Track 20 to 40 buyer questions across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, and go after the sources that keep naming your competitors.
