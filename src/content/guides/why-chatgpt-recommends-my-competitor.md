**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 2026.**

It's one of the most frustrating things to see: you ask ChatGPT for the best product in your space and it names a competitor, sometimes one with worse reviews or a fraction of your sales. It isn't random, and it isn't about size.

## It's not about who's bigger

In my [test of 48 buyer questions](/reports/who-chatgpt-recommends-2026), 27 answers named at least one growing Shopify brand, and 14 of 44 top picks were one of them. AI isn't ranking brands by revenue. It's picking the best evidence for that specific question.

## The four usual reasons

### 1. The sources mention them, not you

ChatGPT showed a source or store link in 45 of 48 product answers. If the review site, the "best of" article and the Reddit thread behind the answer all name your competitor, so will the AI. Check the cited sources for your question. Usually you'll find your competitor in two or three of them and yourself in none.

### 2. Their page answers the question and yours doesn't

If the shopper asks for "a vitamin C serum that doesn't oxidize" and your competitor's page explains how their formula stays stable while yours talks about glow, the AI goes with the page that answers. I see this constantly: the brand with the better product loses because its page never says the thing.

### 3. They own the broader question

Many brands win the narrow question that matches their exact product and lose the broad one shoppers ask first. You may be the top answer for "best plastic free serum bar" and missing from "best zero waste skincare." [Wording changes the top pick](/reports/how-question-wording-changes-ai-recommendations) in most questions, so you need to know which version your buyers use.

### 4. The AI has your facts wrong

AI gets ratings wrong surprisingly often. All three AIs got the rating right for only [7 of 22 products](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026) I asked about directly. If AI thinks you have 3.9 stars when you have 4.7, you'll fall below the [4.0 bar](/reports/star-ratings-reviews-ai-answers) and your competitor won't.

## How to find your exact reason

1. Write down 10 questions a buyer would ask before finding you.
2. Run each one in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode.
3. For every answer that names your competitor, list the sources cited.
4. Compare their product page with yours on the specific facts in the question.
5. Ask each AI directly about your product's price and rating, and check the answers.
