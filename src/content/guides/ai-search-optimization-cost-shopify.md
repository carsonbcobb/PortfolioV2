**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 2026.**

Most agencies hide this behind a sales call. Here's what drives the price, what you should get at each level, and my actual prices as a reference point.

## What drives the price

**1. Catalog size.** Product pages and product data get fixed one product at a time. A 10 product skincare line is a different job from a 400 product supplement store.

**2. Off site work.** Getting your brand into the review sites, articles and threads AI cites is slow, manual work. It's the biggest cost difference between a cheap offer and a serious one, and it matters, because ChatGPT showed a source in [45 of 48 product answers](/reports/sources-chatgpt-cites-2026) I tested.

**3. Tracking.** How many buyer questions, across how many AI tools, how often. Tracking one AI with a handful of questions is cheap and nearly useless, since [rewording a question changes the answer](/reports/how-question-wording-changes-ai-recommendations).

## The three ways it's sold

### A one time audit

Shows how often you're recommended, who's named instead, which sources the AI trusts, what it gets wrong about your products, and a ranked fix list. **Mine is $1,500**, credited toward a build or first retainer month if you start within 30 days.

### A one time build

Someone actually fixes your store: product data for every active product, product pages for your top sellers, crawler access, and conversion on the pages AI sends people to. **Mine is fixed price by active product count:** $3,500 for 1 to 10 products, $5,000 for 11 to 50, $7,500 for 51 to 500, and from $10,000 above that. That includes two revision rounds, 14 days of fixes after launch and a retest at 30 days.

### Ongoing work

Monthly tracking, new fixes as answers shift, and off site outreach. **Mine runs $2,500 to $7,500 a month** (Core, Growth and Full), with a 3 month minimum. Off site outreach starts at Growth.

## What cheap offers usually leave out

- **Only one AI tracked**, usually ChatGPT. ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Mode only [fully agree on 23% of brands](/reports/chatgpt-gemini-ai-mode-brand-overlap-2026).
- **An app instead of real fixes.** Product data injected by script is often [invisible to AI crawlers](/reports/ai-crawler-access-shopify).
- **llms.txt as the deliverable.** Shopify already gives you one, and [no major AI says it reads it](/reports/does-llms-txt-do-anything).
- **No off site plan.** On site fixes keep the AI accurate. Outside sources decide most of who gets picked.

## Is it worth it?

Shoppers who come from AI assistants arrive already deciding, and in my experience they convert at a much higher rate than other traffic. For a brand doing $20,000 a month or more with products people compare before buying, the math usually works within a few months.
