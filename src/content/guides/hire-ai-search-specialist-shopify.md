**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 2026.**

Every agency now sells "AI search." Some of them are doing real work. Some renamed their SEO package. Here are the questions I'd ask if I were hiring someone for my own brand, and what a good answer sounds like.

## 1. Which AI tools do you track?

**Good answer:** ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode at minimum. They [agree less than you'd expect](/reports/chatgpt-gemini-ai-mode-brand-overlap-2026): only 23% of brands named came from all three.

**Red flag:** "We optimize for AI" with no specific tools named.

## 2. How do you measure whether it's working?

**Good answer:** a fixed set of real buyer questions for your products, run on a schedule, tracking how often you're named, where you rank in the answer, and who's named instead.

**Red flag:** reporting only Google rankings or traffic, or one screenshot of a single question. One question tells you almost nothing, since [rewording it changes the top pick](/reports/how-question-wording-changes-ai-recommendations) more often than not.

## 3. Which questions will you test?

**Good answer:** questions written the way shoppers ask AI, short and long, at the product level. AI queries are much longer than Google searches, and the long ones often name a need ("fragrance free, for sensitive skin, under $40").

**Red flag:** a list of keywords.

## 4. Do you work in our theme code or install an app?

**Good answer:** either can be fine, but they should be able to explain exactly what changes on your product pages and why, and leave you with something you own.

**Red flag:** an app that injects product data with JavaScript. AI crawlers often [don't run scripts](/reports/ai-crawler-access-shopify), and a second product data block from an app is a common cause of [wrong ratings in AI answers](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026).

## 5. What do you do about outside sources?

**Good answer:** they find which review sites, articles, Reddit threads and videos the AI cites for your questions, and have a plan to get your brand into them. ChatGPT showed a source in [45 of 48 product answers](/reports/sources-chatgpt-cites-2026) I tested.

**Red flag:** on site fixes only, with no view of what the AI is reading elsewhere.

## 6. What's your take on llms.txt?

**Good answer:** it's harmless and not a priority. Shopify already gives [95% of stores a default one](/reports/does-llms-txt-do-anything), and no major AI search product says it reads the file.

**Red flag:** llms.txt sold as the main deliverable.

## 7. Will you also fix conversion?

AI referred shoppers arrive with a specific promise in mind, and they convert well when the page backs it up. Someone who only works on visibility will send those shoppers to a page that wasn't built for them.

**Good answer:** they look at the product page the AI sends people to and make sure it confirms what the AI said.

## 8. Who actually does the work?

**Good answer:** a named senior person you can talk to.

**Red flag:** a sales lead you never hear from again after kickoff.

## Agency or specialist?

For most Shopify brands doing under a few hundred thousand dollars a month, a senior specialist who does the work directly is the better fit. You get the person who knows your account, without paying for layers. A larger agency makes sense when you need a big content and PR operation running alongside.
