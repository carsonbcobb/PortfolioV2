**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16, 2026.**

The meta description is the short summary under your page title in Google results, and in many link previews. On a product page it's often the first description of the product a shopper reads.

Shopify fills it in for you if you don't. I checked one product page from each of 104 Shopify brand stores to see how many stores wrote their own.

## The short version

- **54% are just the product description.** 56 of 104 started with the same text as the product description.
- **27% are cut off at exactly 320 characters.** 28 of 104, Shopify's automatic length.
- **7% are empty.** 7 of 104.
- **Most are too long for Google.** The median was 226 characters, and 60% ran over 160.
- **Only 3 of 104 put a promotion in it.**

## What I found

| | Product pages | Share of 104 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starts with the product description text | 56 | 54% |
| Over 160 characters | 62 | 60% |
| Exactly 320 characters (Shopify's automatic cut) | 28 | 27% |
| Empty | 7 | 7% |
| Under 70 characters | 3 | 3% |
| Mentions a promotion or code | 3 | 3% |

Median length: 226 characters.

## What default descriptions look like

When the meta description is the opening of the product description, it looks like these examples from the data, described rather than quoted:

- A bundle description that lists every item in the bundle, cut off mid sentence.
- A promotional note about shipping and a coupon code, because that was the first line of the description.
- A holiday bundle whose description leads with its price and savings.
- A marketing opener that never says what the product is.

None of these are wrong, exactly. But they aren't written for the person deciding whether to click, and they end with "..." in the middle of a thought.

## Why it matters now

Google rewrites many meta descriptions anyway, but it starts from what you give it. A deliberate, accurate summary gives you control over the one line that describes your product in search results, in link previews, and to tools that read search results, including AI. See [Google rankings vs AI Mode](/reports/google-rankings-vs-ai-mode-2026).

It's also a consistency problem. When the meta description, the product description, the structured data and your product feed each say something slightly different, the AI reading them has to pick. See [what AI gets wrong about your products](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026).

## A simple formula

What it is + who it's for + one proof point, in about 150 characters.

"Magnesium glycinate, 120 capsules. Gentle on the stomach, for sleep and muscle recovery. Third party tested, 60 day guarantee."

## What to do with this

1. **Write descriptions for your top 20 products by hand.** Search engine listing, in each product's page in Shopify admin.
2. **Keep it around 150 characters.** Say the important part first.
3. **Lead with what the product is.** Product type, size or count, the key ingredient or material.
4. **Add one proof point.** A rating, a guarantee, a certification.
5. **Keep promotions out.** They expire, and the description stays in search results long after.
6. **Fix your product description's first line too.** It's the fallback for every product you don't write by hand.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 107 Shopify brand stores, 17 to 18 in each of six product areas (supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home, apparel), drawn at random from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores. Client stores were excluded.
- **Product pages:** one available product per store, the first in its public product list, loaded November 16, 2026. 104 product pages loaded.
- **Measures:** the length of the meta description tag; whether its first 40 characters appeared in the first 60 characters of the product's description; whether it was exactly 320 characters, the length of Shopify's automatic description; and keyword rules for promotions ("free shipping", "% off", "code").
- **Limits:** one product page per store. A store may have written descriptions for some products and not others.

No store or brand is named in this report.
