**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16 and 17, 2026.**

The homepage title and meta description are the shortest description of your brand that you control. Google shows them, AI tools read them, and they often end up as the one line summary of what your company is. I checked how 609 Shopify brand homepages handle the basics.

## The short version

- **55% of homepage titles are 30 to 60 characters.** 18% are under 30, usually just the brand name.
- **45% of meta descriptions run over 160 characters** and get cut off in search results. 4% have none.
- **Only 54% have exactly one H1.** 16% have none and 30% have two or more.
- **Canonical tags and language are near universal.** Shopify handles those.
- **84% set an Open Graph image.**

## Why the basics still matter

A title that's just the brand name works for people who already know you. For everyone else, and for any machine trying to summarize what you sell, "Brand | Plant Based Protein Powder and Bars" says far more than "Brand". The same goes for the meta description: it's one of the few places you write a plain summary of the company.

H1s are a theme problem more than a writing problem. Themes often put the logo in an H1, or turn every slideshow heading into one. A homepage with five H1s has no main heading.

## What to do with this

1. **Write the title as brand plus what you sell.** 30 to 60 characters.
2. **Rewrite the meta description in under 160 characters.** What you make, who it's for, one reason to choose you.
3. **Check your H1 count.** View source and search for "<h1". Keep one, and make it say what you sell.
4. **Set an Open Graph image** in theme settings so shared links don't pull a random image.

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads. 609 homepages were readable.
- **When:** November 16 and 17, 2026.
- **How:** each homepage's source as the server first sends it, before JavaScript runs. Lengths are in characters after decoding.
- **Limits:** H1s added or removed by JavaScript weren't counted.
