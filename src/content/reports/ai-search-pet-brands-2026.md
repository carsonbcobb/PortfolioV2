**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight pet shopping questions, including fresh dog food brands, training treats for puppies, a harness that does not press on the neck and calming chews for anxious dogs, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 41 pet brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **10 of 41 pet stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 24%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 4 brands per answer.** Gemini 6, Google AI Mode 5.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 2 per question were named by all three.
- **71 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## What stands out

Pet brands did best in AI answers. 10 of the 41 pet stores in my dataset were named at least once, 24%, more than twice the overall rate. They're also the biggest publishers: the median pet store has 142 blog articles, double the overall median, and 68% posted in the last 90 days.

Pet product pages lean on guarantees, 71% against 33% overall, which makes sense for owners nervous about a new food or chew. What they miss is the detail AI uses to narrow a pick: breed size, age, weight range and sensitivities. In my research, AI asked about pet size and age before choosing. Only 8% of pet product pages put return or shipping terms in structured data.

## What to do with this

1. **State size, age and weight ranges** on every product page and in variant names.
2. **Keep publishing on owner questions.** Pet brands that publish are the ones AI knows.
3. **Back the guarantee with specifics.** Window, process, and what counts.
4. **Add a vet reviewed or tested claim only if it's true,** and say who reviewed it.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight pet shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 41 pet stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
