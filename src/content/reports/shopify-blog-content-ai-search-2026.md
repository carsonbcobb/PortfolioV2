**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16, 2026.**

A Shopify blog used to be mostly for Google. Now it's also one of the places AI looks when a shopper asks "how do I choose", "how do I use it" or "is X better than Y". The question is whether stores are writing what shoppers ask.

I read the article list of 107 Shopify brand stores, 16,726 articles in total, and sorted them by type and by when they were last updated.

## The short version

- **85% of stores have a blog.** 91 of 107. The median blog had 106 articles.
- **Only 52% updated anything in the last 90 days.** 56 of 107.
- **15 stores have a blog that hasn't changed in a year.**
- **17% of articles answer a question.** How to, what is, why, guides.
- **Only 6% are "best" lists and 2% are comparisons.** The formats shoppers ask AI for most.

## Who's publishing

| | Stores | Share of 107 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Have published articles | 91 | 85% |
| Article updated in the last 90 days | 56 | 52% |
| Article updated in the last year | 76 | 71% |
| Have a blog, nothing updated in a year | 15 | 14% |
| No articles at all | 16 | 15% |

## What they publish

| Article type | Articles | Share of 16,726 | Stores with at least one |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How to, what is, why, guides | 2,854 | 17% | 82 |
| Best of and top lists | 1,048 | 6% | 67 |
| Comparisons (vs, alternatives) | 375 | 2% | 54 |

The other three quarters of articles were things like recipes, announcements, founder stories, lifestyle content and product launches. Those can build an audience, but they rarely answer the specific question a shopper types into ChatGPT before buying.

## The gap: comparisons

Comparison questions are everywhere in AI shopping: "X vs Y", "is this better than that", "alternatives to". When a store doesn't answer them, AI answers them from review sites, roundups and Reddit, which may not describe your product the way you would. See [where AI sends shoppers](/reports/where-ai-sends-shoppers-2026).

Half the stores I checked had no comparison article at all. Across all 16,726 articles, only 375 compared anything.

## Fresh matters, but not the way you think

AI and Google both favor information that looks current, and an article last touched in 2022 about a product that's changed since is a liability. But publishing more often isn't the fix. Updating the 10 articles that answer real buying questions, with current prices, ingredients and product names, does more than adding 10 new lifestyle posts.

## What to do with this

1. **List the questions shoppers ask before buying.** From support tickets, reviews, Search Console and asking AI yourself.
2. **Write one article per real question.** "How to choose a creatine", "Is merino warmer than synthetic", "Product A vs product B".
3. **Write the comparison pieces nobody else will.** Fair, specific, with a table. Include where the alternative wins.
4. **Update before you add.** Refresh your top articles with current products and prices, and update the date when you do.
5. **Link articles to products.** Every buying guide should send the reader to the right product page.
6. **Prune what's wrong.** Old articles about discontinued products should be updated, merged or redirected.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 107 Shopify brand stores, 17 to 18 in each of six product areas (supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home, apparel), drawn at random from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores. Client stores were excluded.
- **Articles:** every article URL in each store's article sitemaps (`sitemap_articles`), read November 16, 2026, with the sitemap's last modified date.
- **Types:** classified by the article's URL with keyword rules: "how to", "what is", "why", "guide" and similar for questions; "best" and "top" for lists; "vs", "versus", "compare" and "alternative" for comparisons.
- **Limits:** the last modified date shows when an article was last changed, not when it was first published. Classifying by URL misses articles whose URL doesn't describe them, so the question and comparison counts are likely a little low.

No store or brand is named in this report.
