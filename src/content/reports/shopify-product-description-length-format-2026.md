**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16 and 17, 2026.**

The product description is the one block of text on a product page that a brand fully controls, and it's the text Shopify passes to its catalog, Google Shopping feeds and AI shopping tools. I measured the length and format of descriptions across up to 250 products per store in 600 Shopify brand stores.

## The short version

- **The median store's typical description is 61 words.** A quarter are under 40 words.
- **9% of stores have most descriptions under 20 words.** That's one or two sentences.
- **29% never use headings or lists** in any description.
- **30% use headings or lists in at least half** their descriptions.
- **Length didn't predict AI recommendations.** Brands AI named had a median of 56 words, versus 62 for brands it didn't.

## Length isn't the point

I compared the 65 stores in this dataset that ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI Mode named in at least one of 48 shopping answers with the 552 they didn't. Their descriptions weren't longer. What stood out instead was content elsewhere on the site, like testing claims and comparison content on product pages. More in [what AI named Shopify brands have in common](/reports/what-ai-named-shopify-brands-have-in-common-2026).

A 60 word description can be enough if it states the facts a shopper filters on. A 300 word one can say nothing if it's all mood.

## What to do with this

1. **Lead with the facts.** What it is, who it's for, the size or count, the key ingredient or material.
2. **Use short headings or lists** for ingredients, how to use, and what's included.
3. **Fix the shortest ones first.** Sort your catalog by description length and rewrite anything under 20 words.
4. **Use the words shoppers use.** If they ask for "fragrance free," write "fragrance free," not "clean scent profile."
5. **Keep it consistent with the rest of the page.** A description that disagrees with the size chart or ingredients list creates the kind of conflict AI repeats.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads. 600 had a readable public product feed.
- **When:** November 16 and 17, 2026.
- **How:** up to 250 products per store from the public product feed. Words were counted in each description after removing HTML; each store's median was used. Headings or lists means the description HTML contained an H2 to H4 heading or a list item.
- **AI comparison:** 48 shopping questions asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode in November 2026, eight per product area.
- **Limits:** descriptions only. Many stores put more product content in tabs, metafields or app blocks outside the description.

No store or brand is named in this report.
