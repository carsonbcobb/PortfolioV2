**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 17, 2026.**

Most carts don't become orders. The research on why is consistent, and most of the reasons can be answered before the shopper ever reaches checkout. Here are the numbers that hold up, with sources, and how 599 Shopify brand stores handle them in my own scan.

## The rate

| Statistic | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average documented cart abandonment rate | 70.22% | Baymard Institute, 50 studies, updated October 2026 |
| Range across those studies | 55% to 84% | Same |

## Why shoppers abandon

US shoppers who abandoned during checkout in the past three months, excluding those who were just browsing. Shoppers could give more than one reason.

| Reason | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Extra costs too high (shipping, tax, fees) | 40% |
| Delivery too slow | 20% |
| Didn't trust the site with card details | 19% |
| Site wanted me to create an account | 18% |
| Checkout too long or complicated | 17% |
| Website errors or crashes | 17% |
| Returns policy not good enough | 13% |
| Couldn't see the total cost up front | 12% |
| Card declined | 10% |
| Not enough payment methods | 9% |

Source: [Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate-statistics).

## Checkout benchmarks

| Statistic | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form elements in the average US checkout | 23.48 (14.88 form fields) | Baymard |
| Ideal number of form elements | 12 to 14 (7 to 8 fields) | Baymard |
| Potential conversion lift from better checkout design, average large site | 35.26% | Baymard |
| Lost US and EU orders recoverable through checkout design | $260 billion | Baymard |

Shopify's checkout handles much of the form side already. What Shopify brands control is what shoppers know before they get there.

## What 599 Shopify brands answer before checkout

| Abandonment reason | What answers it on the product page | Product pages that do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra costs | Shipping threshold or cost stated near the price | See [shipping policy benchmarks](/reports/shopify-shipping-policy-benchmarks-2026) |
| Slow delivery | A delivery estimate | 11% |
| Trust and returns | A guarantee or free returns line | 34% |
| Payment options | Express checkout buttons | 43% |
| Payment options | Installments | 68% offer Shop Pay Installments; 5% show the message in the page source |

On the cart page itself, only 23% of stores showed product suggestions when the cart was empty, and 15% mentioned free shipping there.

More on the product page side in [the Shopify buy box](/reports/shopify-product-page-buy-box-2026).

## What to do with this

1. **Show total cost early.** Shipping threshold and cost on the product page and in the cart, before checkout.
2. **Add a delivery estimate** on the product page.
3. **State the guarantee and returns** in one line under the add to cart button.
4. **Keep express checkout visible on mobile**, and test it against your average order value.
5. **Don't force accounts.** Shopify's checkout supports guest checkout; keep it on.
6. **Make the empty cart useful**, with best sellers and the free shipping threshold.

## How this was compiled

- **Outside figures:** Baymard Institute's cart abandonment research, as published on its site and updated October 28, 2026. Survey percentages are as Baymard reports them.
- **My figures:** 599 Shopify brand stores in six product areas, scanned November 17, 2026, reading the product page of the first available product and the cart page as first sent by the server. Shop Pay Installments came from store settings read November 1, 2026 (585 stores).

No store or brand is named in this report.
