**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight food and drink shopping questions, including low sugar cereal brands, hot sauce brands to give as a gift, the best coffee subscription and gluten free granola, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 154 food and drink brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **19 of 154 food and drink stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 12%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 5 brands per answer.** Gemini 8, Google AI Mode 6.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 2 per question were named by all three.
- **96 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## What stands out

Food and drink is the most open area in AI answers. The engines named 96 different brands across eight questions, more than any other area, and Gemini named a median of 8 per answer. 19 of the 154 food and drink stores in my dataset were named at least once, 12%.

The store side is the least complete. 25% of homepages have no structured data at all, 80% of product pages have Product structured data against 86% overall, and only 15% carry a GTIN, even though most packaged food has a barcode. Food product pages also skip the practical details: 40% mention returns, and few say who a product suits. Gift questions, like hot sauce gifts, are a big opening, since AI answers them with lists.

## What to do with this

1. **Put the barcode in Shopify.** Most packaged food has one. Fill the Barcode field on every variant.
2. **State diet facts plainly.** Gluten free, low sugar, vegan, certified organic, in text and in tags.
3. **Build gift pages that answer gift questions.** Who it's for, price range, what's in the box.
4. **Add structured data to the homepage** if your theme doesn't output it.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight food and drink shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 154 food and drink stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
