**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16, 2026.**

The announcement bar is the first line of text on almost every page of a Shopify store. It's seen more than any headline, product description or banner. Most stores fill it with whatever the last promotion was.

I loaded the homepage of 107 Shopify brand stores in six product areas and read the announcement bar. 63 showed readable text. Here's what they said.

## The short version

- **Free shipping dominates.** 35 of 63 bars (56%) promoted free shipping, almost always with a threshold.
- **Sales come second.** 22 of 63 (35%) promoted a sale or discount, 12 of them for Black Friday in mid November.
- **The trust messages are missing.** Only 2 bars mentioned returns. Only 3 used social proof like review counts.
- **A quarter of bars stack offers.** 25 of 63 crammed several messages into one line or rotated between them.
- **44 of 107 homepages had no readable message** when the page loaded, either no bar or an empty one.

## What the bars said

| Message | Stores | Share of 63 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free shipping (with or without a threshold) | 35 | 56% |
| Sale, percent off or bundle savings | 22 | 35% |
| Black Friday or Cyber Monday | 12 | 19% |
| New product or limited edition | 11 | 17% |
| Free gift or sample with purchase | 7 | 11% |
| Holiday shop or gift sets | 7 | 11% |
| Discount code to type | 5 | 8% |
| Social proof (reviews, customers, rankings) | 3 | 5% |
| Pay later, HSA or FSA | 3 | 5% |
| Returns or guarantee | 2 | 3% |
| Subscription offer | 2 | 3% |

Messages can fall in more than one row.

## Everyone says the same thing

When more than half of stores use the bar for free shipping, the message stops working as a reason to choose you. It's expected. Free shipping still belongs somewhere visible, but it's become the price of entry, not an edge.

The messages that answer a shopper's real hesitation, "what if it doesn't work for me?" and "is this brand legit?", were almost absent. 2 stores mentioned returns. 3 used social proof. None used a delivery cutoff like "order by 2pm, ships today", which is one of the most persuasive messages during the holidays.

## Stacked and rotating bars

25 of 63 bars stacked or rotated several messages. On a phone, a single line that reads "free shipping over $50 | 20% off sitewide | new flavors | refer a friend" is cut off after the first item or two. A rotating bar is worse in a different way: each message shows for a few seconds, and the shopper usually sees only the first before they scroll.

The median message was 68 characters, which fits on most phones. Stacked bars ran much longer.

## The threshold has to match the policy

33 stores advertised a free shipping threshold in dollars. Only 12 of their shipping policies stated the same threshold. See [Shopify shipping policies](/reports/shopify-shipping-policy-benchmarks-2026). A shopper who checks, or an AI agent reading your policy, should find the same number.

## What to do with this

1. **Pick one job for the bar.** The message most likely to remove a reason not to buy, shown first.
2. **Test a trust message against free shipping.** "60 day money back guarantee" or "Free returns" is rare enough to stand out. Run it as an A/B test, with conversion rate as the measure.
3. **Add a cutoff during gifting season.** "Order by Dec 18 for Christmas delivery" answers the question every December shopper has.
4. **Show progress, not just the threshold.** In the cart, "You're $12 away from free shipping" does more than the bar ever will. Set the threshold with the [free shipping threshold calculator](/tools/free-shipping-calculator).
5. **One or two messages, not five.** If you rotate, slow it down and put the important one first.
6. **Remove old promotions the day they end.** An expired code in the bar costs trust.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 107 Shopify brand stores, 17 to 18 in each of six product areas (supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home, apparel), drawn at random from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores. Client stores were excluded.
- **Announcement bars:** the text of the announcement bar element on each homepage, loaded November 16, 2026 in a desktop browser. Bars showing only cookie notices, accessibility prompts or a default "Welcome to our store" were excluded, leaving 63 with a readable message.
- **Classification:** keyword rules for each message type (for example "free shipping", "% off", "Black Friday", "return", "reviews"), checked by hand.
- **Limits:** some bars load or change after the page loads, or differ by country and device. This is one snapshot during the run up to Black Friday, so sale messages are higher than they would be in a quieter month.

No store or brand is named in this report.
