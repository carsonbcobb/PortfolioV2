**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 1 to 17, 2026.**

Payment options and rewards both promise the same thing: fewer abandoned carts and more repeat orders. I checked what Shopify brand stores actually offer.

## The short version

- **68% offer Shop Pay Installments.** 398 of 585 stores.
- **28% also load Afterpay, Klarna or Affirm code.** 172 of 609 homepages.
- **11% load two or more of those three.**
- **Beauty and apparel lead** buy now pay later, at 43% and 42%.
- **Only 10% load LoyaltyLion or Smile.**

## What this says

Shop Pay Installments covers most stores without a separate app, so the extra providers are a choice. Beauty and apparel add them most, which fits their higher priced baskets. Food and drink stores mostly don't, which fits order values where splitting a payment doesn't change much.

Loyalty is the surprise. Repeat purchase is the business model for most consumable brands, yet 90% load no dedicated loyalty app. Many rely on subscriptions instead: 33% of supplements stores loaded Recharge code and 17% Skio.

## What to do with this

1. **Show installments where the price is decided.** On the product page near the price, not only at checkout.
2. **Don't stack providers without a reason.** Each adds code and a badge. Pick the one your shoppers use.
3. **Decide what drives your repeat orders.** Subscription, loyalty points or reorder emails, and make one of them excellent.
4. **Check the numbers.** Compare conversion and order value for shoppers who use installments against those who don't.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads. 609 homepages were readable.
- **When:** Shop Pay Installments was read from store settings on November 1, 2026 (585 of these stores). App code was read from homepages on November 16 and 17, 2026.
- **How:** provider and app code detected from known script and code patterns in each homepage's source.
- **Limits:** code on the page doesn't prove the option shows at checkout. Providers enabled only inside Shopify Payments without on site messaging may be missed.

No store or brand is named in this report.
