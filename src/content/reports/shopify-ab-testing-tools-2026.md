**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16 and 17, 2026.**

Most product page changes on Shopify go live on a hunch. A/B testing tools let a store show two versions to real shoppers and keep the one that sells more. I checked how many Shopify brand homepages load one.

## The short version

- **33% load an A/B testing tool.** 199 of 609 homepages.
- **Intelligems leads at 19%**, then Shoplift at 9%.
- **Supplements and apparel test most**, at 48% and 47%.
- **Food and drink tests least**, at 17%.
- **4% load two or more testing tools.**

## What this says

Intelligems' lead fits how Shopify brands test now: price, shipping thresholds and offers, not just button colors. Supplements stores, which live on subscription and bundle offers, lean on it hardest. Shoplift, which tests theme templates and page layouts, is spread more evenly.

Two thirds of brands load no testing tool. Many are big enough to test. The usual blockers are traffic split across too many products, no one owning the testing plan, and tests stopped before the result means anything.

AI makes this more pressing, not less. Shoppers referred by AI arrive with a specific question already half answered and land deep in the site. Shopify said in Q3 2026 that AI referred shoppers convert 64% better than organic search visitors ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/shopify-revenue-gains-32-to-3-7bn-as-ai-referred-orders-rise-3-3x/)). The product page they land on has to confirm what AI told them, and testing is how you find out whether it does.

## What to do with this

1. **Start where the traffic is.** Your top three product pages and the cart.
2. **Test offers and clarity before design.** Price presentation, bundles, shipping threshold, what the page says above the fold.
3. **Run one test per page at a time**, and let it run long enough to trust.
4. **Remove testing code you no longer use.** It still loads on every page.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads. 609 homepages were readable.
- **When:** November 16 and 17, 2026.
- **How:** testing tool code detected from known script and code patterns in each homepage's source.
- **Limits:** code on the page doesn't prove a test is running. Tools loaded only through a tag manager or app embed after load may be missed, and Shopify's own theme tools aren't detected.

No store or brand is named in this report.
