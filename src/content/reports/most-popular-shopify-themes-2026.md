**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 17, 2026.**

Theme market share lists usually count every Shopify store, including millions of tiny ones. This one counts established DTC brands only: 599 Shopify brand stores in supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home and apparel. I read the theme each store's homepage reports.

## The short version

- **Dawn leads, at 13%.** 80 of 599 stores. Shopify's free theme is the default for a reason.
- **Then Impulse (3%), Horizon (3%), Broadcast (2%) and Prestige (2%).**
- **Brands run 263 different themes.** The top 10 cover only about a third of stores.
- **A third run a theme nobody else in the sample uses.** 197 stores, usually custom builds.
- **Shopify's free themes run 22% of stores.**
- **Only 24% run a theme still linked to its Theme Store listing.**

## Old versions are common

Debut, Shopify's older free theme, still runs 12 stores, years after Shopify moved to its current theme architecture. Of the 80 Dawn stores, 33 run version 15 and 30 run version 10 or older.

That matters because a theme linked to its Theme Store listing can take the developer's updates, which bring speed, accessibility and structured data fixes. An uploaded copy or a heavily edited theme usually can't, so it falls behind quietly. In this sample, 76% of stores run a theme that isn't linked to a Theme Store listing.

## What this means

The theme matters less than what's done with it. Dawn stores range from very fast to very slow, depending on apps and sections. But an old, disconnected theme is a common root cause of the problems I see most in audits: images loaded the slow way, missing structured data, and accessibility gaps. More on those in [image loading on Shopify](/reports/shopify-image-loading-speed-2026) and [Shopify accessibility statistics](/reports/shopify-accessibility-statistics-2026).

## What to do with this

1. **Check your theme and version.** Online Store, Themes, then the version under the theme name.
2. **If you're two or more major versions behind, plan an upgrade.** Test it on a duplicate first.
3. **If your theme is custom, ask who maintains it.** Someone has to apply the platform changes a Theme Store developer would ship.
4. **Don't switch themes for looks alone.** Switch when the current one blocks speed, structured data or conversion features you need.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 599 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (118), beauty (125), food and drink (152), pet (41), home (72) and apparel (91). These are the stores from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores that were readable on the day, with client stores excluded.
- **When:** November 17, 2026.
- **How:** the theme name, version and Theme Store ID each store's homepage reports in its page source. Free themes counted: Dawn, Horizon, Debut, Sense, Refresh, Craft, Studio and the other themes Shopify publishes at no cost.
- **Limits:** a store can rename its theme. I used the theme's internal name, which survives renaming, but a custom theme built on Dawn may report a different name.

No store or brand is named in this report.
