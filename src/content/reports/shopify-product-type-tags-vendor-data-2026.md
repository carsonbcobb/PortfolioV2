**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16 and 17, 2026.**

Before any AI reads your product page, it can read your catalog. Shopify's public product feed, its own catalog for AI shopping and every sales channel start with the same few fields: title, product type, vendor and tags. They're short, they're structured, and they're often left as defaults.

I checked those fields across up to 250 products per store in 600 Shopify brand stores.

## The short version

- **The median store fills product type on 94% of products.** But 8% of stores leave it empty everywhere.
- **The median product has 5 tags.** 11% of stores use no tags at all.
- **Half of stores use more than one vendor name.** In a store that only sells its own brand, that can mean the same brand spelled different ways.
- **The median typical title is 26 characters.** 17% of stores are under 20.
- **3% of stores write most titles in all capitals.**

## Why these fields matter for AI

Shopify said in its Q3 2026 results that it has converted more than a billion product pages into structured catalog data, and that searches running on that catalog convert at twice the rate of general AI search ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/shopify-revenue-gains-32-to-3-7bn-as-ai-referred-orders-rise-3-3x/)). Product type and vendor are among the cleanest signals in that data. An empty product type means a machine has to work out what the product is from the title alone.

Vendor is the quiet one. Many themes output it as the brand in structured data. If half your catalog says "Brand" and the other half says "Brand Inc." or "brand", you're telling machines you're two companies. I covered the wider version of this in [brand name consistency](/reports/shopify-brand-name-consistency-2026).

## What to do with this

1. **Fill product type on every product.** Plain words a shopper would use: "Protein Powder", "Dog Chew", "Linen Sheet Set".
2. **Pick one vendor spelling.** Bulk edit the vendor field so every product you make says the same brand name.
3. **Use tags for real attributes.** Scent, skin type, diet, material, size range. Delete tags nobody filters by.
4. **Put the type in the title when the name doesn't say it.** "Morning" tells nobody anything. "Morning Greens Powder" does.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads. 600 had a readable public product feed.
- **When:** November 16 and 17, 2026.
- **How:** up to 250 products per store from the public product feed. For each store I measured the share of products with a product type, the median tag count, the number of distinct vendor names, and the median title length.
- **Limits:** some stores legitimately sell other brands, so more than one vendor name isn't always a mistake.

No store or brand is named in this report.
