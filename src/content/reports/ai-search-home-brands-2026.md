**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight home shopping questions, including candle brands that smell expensive, the best weighted blanket, eco friendly dishwasher pods and the best insulated tumbler, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 76 home brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **12 of 76 home stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 16%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 4.5 brands per answer.** Gemini 5.5, Google AI Mode 5.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 2 per question were named by all three.
- **61 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## What stands out

Home brands are named at a healthy rate: 12 of 76 stores in my dataset, 16%. Home questions are often single product questions, like the best weighted blanket or the best tumbler, and AI answers them by comparing a handful of known names on specs.

Home stores are the most likely to say who a product is for (51%) and to show video (36%), and they had the most rewritten agents.md files of any area. Their weak spot is weight. The median home homepage sends 701 KB of HTML, second only to apparel. For spec driven questions, the specs need to be readable: dimensions, materials, weight, care, in text and in structured data, not only in images.

## What to do with this

1. **Put specs in text, not just images.** Dimensions, weight, materials, care, capacity.
2. **Answer the comparison question.** How your blanket weight, tumbler size or pod formula compares with the usual options.
3. **Lighten the homepage.** Fewer sections and leftover app code.
4. **Keep the video, add a transcript** or a written summary of what it shows.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight home shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 76 home stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
