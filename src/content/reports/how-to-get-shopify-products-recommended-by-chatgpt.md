**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 17, 2026.**

There's no setting that gets you into ChatGPT. But there's a clear pattern in who it recommends. I built this plan from 144 AI shopping answers across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, repeat tests, fact checks against real product pages, and a scan of 617 Shopify brand stores. Each step links to the research behind it.

## Step 1: Find the questions you should be in

ChatGPT names a median of 4 brands per answer, and its lists barely repeat across questions ([how many brands](/reports/how-many-brands-ai-recommends-2026)). So start with 20 real buyer questions, written by need, not by product name: "best protein powder for a sensitive stomach," not "brand whey isolate." Ask them in ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Mode. Note who's named, and which sources are shown.

## Step 2: Earn the sources ChatGPT reads

ChatGPT showed sources in 45 of 48 answers, led by review and testing sites and magazines, with expert sources in health related areas ([sources ChatGPT cites](/reports/sources-chatgpt-cites-2026)). Take the sources behind the answers you want to be in. Get products to those testers and editors, pitch the gift guides, and make the expert case where it applies.

## Step 3: Put checkable reasons on the product page

AI repeats specific reasons: materials, ingredients, testing, certifications, value ([why AI recommends products](/reports/why-ai-recommends-products-reasons-2026)). The Shopify brands AI named were more likely to mention testing or certification on product pages (40% versus 29%) and to compare options (34% versus 24%) ([what AI named brands have in common](/reports/what-ai-named-shopify-brands-have-in-common-2026)).

## Step 4: Say exactly who each product is for

45% of ChatGPT's "best for" labels named a specific need, and it asked about the shopper's situation in 18 of 48 answers ([best for labels](/reports/how-ai-labels-products-best-for-2026), [follow up questions](/reports/chatgpt-follow-up-questions-shoppers-2026)). Only 38% of Shopify product pages I scanned said who a product is for.

## Step 5: Clean up the product data

Structured data didn't decide who got named, but it decided whether facts were right. Prices were right for all 20 products with product structured data. Ratings went wrong most where a review app added a second Product block, which 21% of Shopify product pages have ([what AI gets wrong](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026)). Fill GTINs (23% do), variant data and return and shipping terms (11% do).

## Step 6: Keep publishing on buyer questions

Named brands had a median of 142 blog articles against 66, and 65% had posted in the last 90 days against 50%. Write the comparison and "which one is right for me" pages reviewers and shoppers need.

## Step 7: Make the landing page close the sale

Shopify said AI referred shoppers convert 64% better than organic search in Q3 2026 ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/shopify-revenue-gains-32-to-3-7bn-as-ai-referred-orders-rise-3-3x/)). They arrive with a specific need already half answered. The product page has to confirm what the answer said, fast, on mobile.

## Step 8: Measure it

Track AI referrals, branded search and direct together, and ask new customers where they first heard of you ([can Shopify brands see their AI traffic](/reports/shopify-analytics-attribution-ai-traffic-2026)).
