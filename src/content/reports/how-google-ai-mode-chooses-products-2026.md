**By Carson Cobb. Research from November 4 to 17, 2026.**

Google AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users in 2026, according to Google ([Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-shares-first-ai-mode-usage-data/)). It looks like Google, but it doesn't pick like Google. Here's what decides its shopping recommendations, from my tests of 48 shopping questions and a side by side comparison of 19 questions in Google and AI Mode.

## 1. Product listings, not articles

In 11 of 19 shopping questions, AI Mode built its answer around product listings, each with a price, a star rating, a review count and a seller. Google says shopping results like these rely on product data in Merchant Center and its Shopping Graph. That makes your product feed as important as your rankings. ([Google rankings vs AI Mode](/reports/google-rankings-vs-ai-mode-2026))

## 2. Not the first page of Google

Only 40% of the sites AI Mode cited ranked on Google's first page for the same question, 36 of 91. Google's number one result was cited for 7 of 19 questions. Reddit, the top Google result for 11 of 19, was cited by AI Mode 4 times.

## 3. Brand stores, including small ones

Brand stores were 16% of Google's first page but 37% of the sites AI Mode cited. 27 of 60 product listings were sold by the brand's own store, and several had fewer than 100 reviews.

## 4. Certifications and specific claims

Across 48 questions, AI Mode cited certifications in 25 answers, against 12 for ChatGPT, and organic claims in 14. It labeled picks for a specific need, like "best for heavy pullers," in 51% of its labels. ([Why AI recommends products](/reports/why-ai-recommends-products-reasons-2026), [best for labels](/reports/how-ai-labels-products-best-for-2026))

## 5. A short list that differs from ChatGPT's

AI Mode named a median of 5 brands per answer. For a typical question, only 2 brands were named by AI Mode, ChatGPT and Gemini alike. Being in one engine's answer says little about the others. ([How many brands](/reports/how-many-brands-ai-recommends-2026))

## 6. Facts from your data, mostly right

AI Mode got the price right for 19 of 24 products I checked, but ratings for only 12 of 22, and it struggled most where a review app added a second copy of the product data. ([What AI gets wrong](/reports/what-ai-gets-wrong-about-products-2026))

## What this means for a brand

| What decides it | What to work on |
| --- | --- |
| Product listings | A complete Merchant Center feed: GTINs, product type, variants, shipping and returns |
| Not Google rank | Track AI Mode separately from rankings |
| Brand stores | Product pages that answer the question directly |
| Certifications | Name certifications and test results plainly |
| Facts | One Product block, ratings in structured data, matching feed and page |

In my scan of 617 Shopify brand stores, only 23% put a GTIN in product structured data and 11% put return or shipping terms there ([agentic commerce readiness](/reports/shopify-agentic-commerce-readiness-2026)). That's the gap AI Mode's product listings expose.

## How this was done

- **AI Mode tests:** 48 shopping questions in November 2026 across six product areas, plus 19 questions compared side by side with Google search on November 14, 2026, from a US session.
- **Fact checks:** AI Mode's stated prices and ratings compared with 24 product pages.
- **Limits:** this measures what AI Mode does, not Google's internal rules, which can change.

No store or brand is named in this report.
