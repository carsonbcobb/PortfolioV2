**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16, 2026.**

AI doesn't decide what to recommend from your website alone. It looks at what others say: review sites, magazines, Reddit, YouTube. Your site can't control those, but it can point to them, and show shoppers the proof that exists.

I checked the homepage of 107 Shopify brand stores for links to social profiles and for press mentions, and compared that with the sources AI used in my earlier research.

## The short version

- **Instagram is nearly universal.** 101 of 107 homepages (94%) link to it.
- **YouTube is linked by fewer than half.** 48 of 107 (45%), even though Google AI Mode cited YouTube in 18 of 48 answers.
- **Press is barely shown.** 15 of 107 homepages (14%) had an "as seen in" or featured in section.
- **A third have a press page.** 34 of 105.
- **The median store links 4 networks.** 6 stores linked none.

From [the sources ChatGPT cites](/reports/sources-chatgpt-cites-2026) and [where AI sends shoppers](/reports/where-ai-sends-shoppers-2026).

The mismatch is clear. Nearly every brand links Instagram, but fewer than half link YouTube and only 14% show their press, even though video and editorial sources are among the most visible in AI answers. A video review or a magazine roundup is evidence AI can find and quote.

## What to do with this

1. **Get on YouTube, even simply.** How to use it, what's inside, honest comparisons. Support creators who review products in your space.
2. **Show your press.** If magazines or review sites have covered you, put the logos and a short quote on the homepage, linked to the article.
3. **Build a press page.** List every review, roundup and article, with links. It's a map for shoppers and for AI.
4. **Link your profiles in your Organization structured data.** As sameAs links, so search engines connect them to your site. See [product schema completeness](/reports/shopify-product-schema-completeness-2026).
5. **Use the same brand name on every profile.** See [is your brand name the same everywhere](/reports/shopify-brand-name-consistency-2026).
6. **Show up in the threads.** In the subreddits where your product comes up, answer questions honestly as the brand.

The outside sources that decide AI recommendations are part of [Close the Gap](/close-the-gap). The [AI Visibility Check](/ai-visibility-check) is a quick first look.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 107 Shopify brand stores, 17 to 18 in each of six product areas (supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home, apparel), drawn at random from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores. Client stores were excluded.
- **Social links:** any link on the homepage to each network's domain, loaded November 16, 2026.
- **Press:** homepage text matching "as seen in", "as seen on", "featured in", "as featured" or "in the press". Press pages: pages in the store's pages sitemap with "press", "media" or "seen" in the URL.
- **AI sources:** from my earlier ChatGPT and Google AI Mode research on 48 buyer questions, linked above.
- **Limits:** homepage links only. A store may link profiles elsewhere. Press sections built as images with no text weren't counted.

No store or brand is named in this report.
