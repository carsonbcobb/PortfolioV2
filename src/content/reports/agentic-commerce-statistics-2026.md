**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 17, 2026.**

Agentic commerce means an AI agent doing part of the shopping: finding, comparing and sometimes buying. The numbers below are the ones that hold up, each with who measured it and when, plus my own scan of how ready Shopify stores are.

## What the numbers say

Agents are real but early. Most AI shopping is still research, and the purchase often happens on the brand's own site. OpenAI's March 2026 change points the same way. What's growing fastest is AI influence on the decision: nearly a third of AI shoppers called it the biggest influence on their last purchase.

For a brand that means two jobs. Be the product the agent finds and understands, with clean data an agent can trust. And make the page the shopper lands on confirm what the agent said.
