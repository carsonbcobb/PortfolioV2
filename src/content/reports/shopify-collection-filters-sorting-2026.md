**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 17, 2026.**

On a collection page, shoppers narrow before they buy. Filters and sorting are how they do it. Without them, a shopper looking for "fragrance free" or "under $30" has to scroll everything or leave. I checked the all products collection on 599 Shopify brand stores.

## The short version

- **62% offer filters.** 50% with Shopify's built in filters, 26% with an app.
- **38% offer none.** No filter code in the page at all.
- **63% offer sorting.**
- **The most common built in filters are custom metafields, price and availability.**
- **Only 5% of all stores filter on Shopify's standard product attributes**, the taxonomy fields Shopify now pushes to its catalog.
- **49% show a collection description.**

## Why it matters

Filters are the on site version of the follow up questions AI asks. In my research, ChatGPT narrowed picks by skin type, budget, pet size or age before recommending ([follow up questions](/reports/chatgpt-follow-up-questions-shoppers-2026)). A store whose collection page can't narrow the same way makes shoppers do the work themselves.

Supplements stores filter least, at 49%, which fits smaller catalogs, but goal based filters (sleep, energy, gut health) are exactly how supplement shoppers think.

The standard product attributes gap is worth watching. Shopify's product taxonomy lets you fill standard fields, like color or dietary preference, that feed both filters and Shopify's catalog for AI shopping. Only 10% of stores with built in filters use them.

## What to do with this

1. **Turn on Shopify's Search and Discovery filters** if you have none.
2. **Filter by how shoppers ask.** Need, skin or hair type, diet, size, scent, price range.
3. **Fill Shopify's standard product attributes** and use them as filters, so the same data feeds your catalog.
4. **Add sorting** with best selling as the default.
5. **Write a short collection description** that says who the collection is for.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 599 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (118), beauty (125), food and drink (152), pet (41), home (72) and apparel (91), from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, client stores excluded.
- **When:** November 17, 2026.
- **Pages:** each store's /collections/all page, which every Shopify store has.
- **How:** page source as first sent by the server. Built in filters were counted from Shopify's filter parameters. Apps were counted from known filter and search app code.
- **Limits:** some stores put filters only on specific collections, not the all products page, and some apps render filters after load.

No store or brand is named in this report.
