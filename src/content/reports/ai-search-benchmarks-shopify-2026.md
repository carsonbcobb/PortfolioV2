**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 16 and 17, 2026.**

This is the reference table. I scanned 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas for everything that affects how AI finds, understands and recommends a store, then set the results side by side. Use your product area's column as the bar to beat.

## Access and agent files

| Measure | All | Supplements | Beauty | Food and drink | Pet | Home | Apparel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stores | 617 | 123 | 127 | 154 | 41 | 76 | 96 |
| Any major AI crawler fully blocked | 1% | 2% | 1% | 0% | 2% | 0% | 4% |
| agents.md responds | 97% | 96% | 98% | 99% | 100% | 96% | 96% |
| agents.md rewritten | 3% | 4% | 2% | 1% | 0% | 8% | 3% |
| llms.txt responds | 98% | 97% | 98% | 99% | 98% | 97% | 98% |

## Reading the table

The access layer is solved for almost everyone, thanks to Shopify's defaults. The differences are in product data and content. Every area has a weak spot: beauty on identifiers and variants, food and drink on structured data and policies, apparel on publishing and duplicate Product blocks, supplements on proof that never reaches the data. Each area's report goes deeper: [supplements](/reports/ai-search-supplement-brands-2026), [beauty](/reports/ai-search-beauty-brands-2026), [food and drink](/reports/ai-search-food-beverage-brands-2026), [pet](/reports/ai-search-pet-brands-2026), [home](/reports/ai-search-home-brands-2026), [apparel](/reports/ai-search-apparel-brands-2026).

Benchmarking your store against these numbers is part of [Close the Gap](/close-the-gap). The [AI Visibility Check](/ai-visibility-check) is a quick first look.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads.
- **When:** November 16 and 17, 2026. AI mentions from 48 shopping questions asked November 4 to 11, 2026.
- **How:** robots.txt, agent files, homepage source, the public product feed and the page source of the first product in each feed, read before JavaScript runs.
- **Limits:** one product page per store, usually a recent product.

No store or brand is named in this report.
