## The caveats

Every one of these figures undercounts. Similarweb notes that AI visits without referrer data are recorded as direct. And the bigger effect never shows as a referral at all: shoppers who read an AI answer, then search your brand on Google or type your URL. In my work with brands, that's how most AI influence shows up.

Growth percentages are also from a small base, and Adobe's figures cover large US retailers that use Adobe Analytics, not every store.
