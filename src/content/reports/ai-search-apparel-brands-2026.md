**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight apparel shopping questions, including leggings that do not go see through, flannel shirts that last, chinos for athletic thighs and wool socks for cold weather, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 96 apparel brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **6 of 96 apparel stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 6%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 4.5 brands per answer.** Gemini 6, Google AI Mode 5.5.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 2 per question were named by all three.
- **73 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## How apparel stores compare

| Measure | Apparel stores | All 617 stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization structured data on the homepage | 82% | 75% |
| Product structured data | 93% | 86% |
| GTIN in product structured data | 40% | 23% |
| Variant data with ProductGroup | 56% | 23% |
| Return or shipping terms in structured data | 14% | 11% |
| Product page has a size or fit guide | 67% | 13% |
| Product page mentions returns | 96% | 67% |
| Median blog articles | 31 | 72 |
| Blog posted in the last 90 days | 52% | 61% |
| No H1 on the homepage | 26% | 16% |
| Any major AI crawler blocked | 4% | 1% |

## What stands out

Apparel stores have the best technical setup and the weakest AI results. They lead every area on product structured data (93%), GTINs (40%) and variant data (56%), and 67% have a size or fit guide. Yet only 6 of 96 apparel stores in my dataset were named, 6%.

The gap is content and coverage. The median apparel store has 31 blog articles, the fewest of any area, and apparel questions are about fit and durability: see through when you squat, fits athletic thighs, actually lasts. Those are answered by reviewers and real wearers, not product data. Apparel is also the area most likely to block AI crawlers, at 4%.

## What to do with this

1. **Write fit and durability content.** How it fits different bodies, how it holds up after 50 washes.
2. **Quote real fit feedback** from reviews on the product page: runs small, true to size.
3. **Don't block AI crawlers** unless you have a specific reason.
4. **Earn coverage from fit reviewers and testers.** That's where apparel answers come from.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight apparel shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 96 apparel stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
