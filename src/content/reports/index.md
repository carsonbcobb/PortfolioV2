Original data on how growing Shopify brands actually run their stores. Every number comes from checking live stores directly, not from surveys or vendor case studies. The current dataset covers **606 US Shopify stores and 121,519 products**, checked November 1, 2026, across supplements, beauty and skincare, food and beverage, pet, home and apparel.

## Reports

### [Black Friday Discount Report 2026](/reports/black-friday-discount-report-2026)
73% of stores already show a crossed out price before Black Friday, and 68% of reorderable brands with subscriptions mark products down deeper than their subscription discount. Before and after numbers coming Black Friday week.

### [Shopify Subscription Benchmarks 2026](/reports/shopify-subscription-benchmarks-2026)
The real subscribe and save discounts, delivery frequencies and subscription apps behind 314 reorderable brands. 10% is the most common discount, and half of brands miss at least one best seller.

### [Holiday Readiness Report 2026](/reports/holiday-readiness-report-2026)
Gift collections, gift cards and bundles by category. 45% of supplement brands have no gift path at all eight weeks before Christmas.

### [Product Page Benchmarks 2026](/reports/shopify-product-page-benchmarks-2026)
Photos, video, description length and pricing style by category. Only 12% of products have video, and pricing style splits sharply by category.

### [Catalog and Stock Report 2026](/reports/shopify-catalog-and-stock-report-2026)
Sold out rates, sold out best sellers and launch pace. 34% of stores have a tenth or more of their catalog sold out.

### [Shopify Tech Stack Report 2026](/reports/shopify-tech-stack-report-2026)
The email, reviews, upsell, analytics and testing tools growing brands load on their homepage, plus themes and free shipping thresholds.

## How the data is collected

Every Shopify store publishes its products, collections, pages, basic settings and subscription plans so the storefront can work. I read that public data directly from each store, and for the tech stack report I load each homepage and record which outside services it calls. Nothing behind a login, no private data and no estimates. Each report explains its definitions and limits in a "How this was done" section.
