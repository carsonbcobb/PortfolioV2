## What the numbers add up to

ChatGPT has become a place where people decide what to buy, then buy wherever they're comfortable. OpenAI's own March 2026 shift toward discovery and merchant checkout fits that. For a brand, the job is to be in the short list, which is a median of four names, and to have the landing page confirm what the answer said.

## How this was compiled

- **Outside figures:** each taken from the source linked, with the date the source gives. Survey sizes are as reported by the source.
- **My figures:** 48 shopping questions asked in ChatGPT in November 2026, and a scan of 617 Shopify brand stores on November 16 and 17, 2026. Methods are in each linked report.

No store or brand is named in this report.
