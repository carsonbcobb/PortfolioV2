**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 17, 2026.**

Beyond products and collections, every store has pages: about, FAQ, contact, and then everything else a brand decides is worth a page. Those pages answer shoppers' questions, give AI something to cite, and carry ads traffic. I read the page sitemap of 595 Shopify brand stores and sorted every page by type.

## The short version

- **The basics are near universal.** About 89%, contact 88%, FAQ 80%.
- **Pages that help shoppers decide are rarer.** Product quiz 40%, ingredients or materials 29%, science or testing 24%, comparison 11%.
- **Half have a reviews page and half an accessibility statement.**
- **The median store has 47 pages.** 22% have 100 or more.

## Why the decision pages matter

These are the pages that answer what shoppers ask AI: which one is right for me, what's in it, is it proven, how does it compare. In my research, AI recommended products for specific, checkable reasons like ingredients, testing and certifications ([why AI recommends products](/reports/why-ai-recommends-products-reasons-2026)), and the Shopify brands AI named had more testing and comparison content on product pages than those it skipped ([what AI named brands have in common](/reports/what-ai-named-shopify-brands-have-in-common-2026)).

A science page with real test results, or a comparison page that's honest about who each product suits, is something a reviewer can link to and AI can cite. Only 24% and 11% of brands have one. I covered comparison pages in more depth in [Shopify comparison pages](/reports/shopify-comparison-pages-2026).

Quizzes are the on site version of AI's follow up questions. Beauty and supplements brands use them most. Food and drink, where "which flavor" and "which roast" are real questions, mostly don't.

## What to do with this

1. **Publish the decision pages first.** Ingredients or materials, testing, and a comparison of your own products.
2. **Add a quiz if shoppers face a real choice.** Skin type, goal, roast, size.
3. **Put a reviews page together** that pulls your best reviews by use case.
4. **Clean up old landing pages.** Hundreds of thin campaign pages dilute what search engines and AI find.
5. **Link the decision pages from product pages**, where shoppers need them.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 599 Shopify brand stores in six product areas, from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, client stores excluded. 595 had a readable sitemap.
- **When:** November 17, 2026.
- **How:** every page address in each store's pages sitemap (up to two sitemap files), sorted into types by keywords in the page address, like "quiz", "finder", "ingredients", "science", "compare" or "vs".
- **Limits:** a page counts by its address, not its content. Stores that put this content on product pages, blog posts or a help center app aren't counted here.

No store or brand is named in this report.
