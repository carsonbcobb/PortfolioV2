**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 and 5, 2026.**

Ask ChatGPT for the best moisturizer and it may give you a list, then ask: "What's your skin type? Dry, oily, combination, normal." Tap one, and the recommendation changes.

Those follow up questions are a gift for anyone selling online. They show exactly which details the AI needs to choose between products. I counted how often each AI asked them, and what they asked about, across the 48 buyer questions from my [Who ChatGPT recommends](/reports/who-chatgpt-recommends-2026) research.

## The short version

- **ChatGPT asked in 38% of answers.** 18 of 48 asked a follow up question or offered to narrow the pick.
- **It often gives tappable choices.** Skin type, budget ranges, dog weight, a child's age, shown as buttons.
- **Google AI Mode asked in 6 of 48.**
- **Gemini rarely asked about the shopper.** It usually ended by suggesting related topics instead.
- **The questions are about the shopper's situation, not the product.** That's the information your product page has to match.

## How often each AI asks

| | Answers with a follow up question or an offer to narrow |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | 18 of 48 (38%) |
| Google AI Mode | 6 of 48 (13%) |
| Gemini | Rarely; it suggested related topics instead |

ChatGPT's follow ups often sat under a heading like "One question that changes my recommendation" or "Two quick questions so I can narrow it down", with answer choices to tap.

## What ChatGPT asked about

| What the question asked | Examples from the answers |
| --- | --- |
| Skin or hair type | Dry, oily, combination, normal; fine or coarse hair |
| Budget | Under $30, $30 to $50, $50 to $100, $100 and up |
| The pet | Breed and weight; small, medium, large, extra large |
| The person it's for | A child's age range; who the gift is for |
| The result wanted | More lift or a natural look; a whole house scent or one room |
| The problem | What kind of anxiety the dog has |
| Where it ships | Where the order will be delivered, to suggest brands that ship there |

## Why this matters for your store

Each follow up question is a filter. When the shopper taps "oily skin", ChatGPT narrows to products it believes suit oily skin. If your product page never says which skin type it's for, or says it vaguely ("for all skin types" on every product), you give the AI nothing to filter on.

The same goes for the rest: size or weight ranges for pet products, age ranges for kids' products, a clear price for budget filters, and shipping regions for where it ships. Those are the facts that keep you in the answer after the shopper narrows it.

They're also the questions your best salespeople already ask in store or in chat. AI is doing the same job, with whatever information it can find.

## What to do with this

1. **Ask ChatGPT about your own products and note the follow ups.** Those are the deciding details for your product type.
2. **Answer each one on the product page.** "Best for oily and combination skin." "Fits dogs 25 to 60 lbs." "Ages 4 to 8." Plain, specific, near the top.
3. **Put the same facts in your product data.** Variant names, product attributes and your Merchant Center feed, so Google's AI can filter on them too.
4. **Show the price clearly.** Budget is one of the most common filters. See [do AI shopping answers show your price](/reports/prices-in-ai-shopping-answers-2026).
5. **Use a product finder or quiz built on the same questions.** If shoppers are being asked these questions in AI, they'll answer them on your site too.
6. **Ask reviewers for the same details.** "I have oily skin" or "my 70 lb lab" in a review backs up your claim.

## How this was done

- **Answers:** the 48 short buyer questions from [Who ChatGPT recommends](/reports/who-chatgpt-recommends-2026), 8 in each of six product areas, asked in ChatGPT on November 4, 2026 and in Gemini and Google AI Mode on November 5, 2026, each in a fresh session.
- **Follow ups:** an answer counted when it asked the shopper a question about their situation or offered to narrow the recommendation ("I can narrow this down", "What's your skin type?"), detected with keyword rules and checked by hand. Offers to explore related topics didn't count.
- **Limits:** 48 questions per AI, one run each. Whether a follow up appears can change from run to run. See [how often AI recommendations change](/reports/how-often-ai-recommendations-change).

No store or brand is named in this report.
