**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 1 to 17, 2026.**

Plenty of Shopify brands ship abroad. Far fewer give search engines and AI tools a clear signal about which page is for which country. That signal is hreflang, a small tag that says "this page is for English in Canada, this one is for English in the UK."

I compared where 585 Shopify brand stores ship with whether their homepages use hreflang.

## The short version

- **The median store ships to 3 countries.** 32% ship to one.
- **18% ship to 150 or more countries.**
- **21% of homepages have hreflang.** 128 of 609.
- **Even among stores shipping to more than 50 countries, only 31% use it.** 45 of 146.
- **Apparel uses it most, at 35%. Food and drink least, at 10%.**

## Why it matters

A store that ships to the UK but shows UK shoppers a US page with dollar prices loses some of them at a glance. Search engines decide which version to show partly from hreflang. Google's AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users globally in 2026, according to Google's own report ([Search Engine Journal, May 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-shares-first-ai-mode-usage-data/)), and Google's AI features build on Google's index. The right local page in the index is the start of the right local answer.

Shopify makes this mostly automatic. Set up a market with its own subfolder or domain and Shopify adds the tags. Most stores that ship widely do it with one domain and one set of URLs, so there's nothing to point to.

## What to do with this

1. **Check where your international orders come from.** If one or two countries matter, give them their own market.
2. **Use Shopify Markets with subfolders or domains** so each market has its own URLs, currency and prices.
3. **Check the result.** View the homepage source and search for "hreflang".
4. **Localize what matters.** Prices, currency, shipping times and returns, not just the language.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 617 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (123), beauty (127), food and drink (154), pet (41), home (76) and apparel (96). This is my full dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads.
- **When:** shipping countries were read from store settings on November 1, 2026 (585 stores). Hreflang was read from homepages on November 16 and 17, 2026 (609 stores).
- **How:** hreflang counted from alternate link tags with an hreflang attribute in each homepage's source.
- **Limits:** shipping to a country doesn't mean a store targets it. Hreflang set only in a sitemap wasn't counted.

No store or brand is named in this report.
