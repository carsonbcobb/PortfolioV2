**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight supplement shopping questions, including the best creatine brands, third party tested protein powder, a multivitamin for picky eater kids and an electrolyte mix for endurance training, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 123 supplement brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **14 of 123 supplement stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 11%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 3 brands per answer.** Gemini 5, Google AI Mode 5.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 2 per question were named by all three.
- **41 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## How supplement stores compare

| Measure | Supplement stores | All 617 stores |

| Organization structured data on the homepage | 79% | 75% |
| Product structured data | 91% | 86% |

## What stands out

Supplement stores are the most thorough on proof. 58% of product pages mention third party testing, certification or clinical work, almost twice the rate of other stores, and 53% explain how to take the product. That fits how AI answers supplement questions: in my research, phrases like "third party tested" came up again and again as the reason a product was recommended.

The gap is that proof rarely reaches the data layer. A testing claim in a paragraph is something a machine has to interpret. GTINs are on 25% of product pages and return terms in structured data on 16%. Two of the eight supplement questions, one about perimenopause and one about focus drinks, got no brand at all from any engine. For those questions, being the brand that explains the ingredient best is the way in.

## What to do with this

1. **Name the test and the lab.** "Third party tested for heavy metals by an ISO accredited lab" beats "rigorously tested".
2. **Publish the certificate.** A results page or batch lookup that reviewers can cite.
3. **Write for the ingredient question too.** Shoppers ask about magnesium or creatine before they ask about brands.
4. **Fill GTINs and return terms** so AI shopping tools can match and compare your products.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight supplement shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 123 supplement stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
