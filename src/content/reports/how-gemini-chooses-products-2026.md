**By Carson Cobb. Research from November 4 to 11, 2026.**

Gemini sits inside Google's apps, Android phones and Chrome, so it reaches shoppers who never open ChatGPT. I asked it the same 48 shopping questions I asked ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, and compared how it picks.

## The short version

- **Gemini names more brands.** A median of 5 per answer and up to 12, against 4 and 8 for ChatGPT.
- **It quotes certifications twice as often as ChatGPT.** 23 of 48 answers versus 12.
- **It rarely shows prices.** 12 of 48 answers, against 38 for ChatGPT.
- **It rarely asks about the shopper.** It suggests related topics instead.
- **It gets ratings wrong more than prices.** Prices right for 22 of 24 products, ratings for 14 of 23.

## What stands out

Gemini behaves like a researcher making a longer list. More brands per answer, more emphasis on certifications and claims, fewer prices. It also agrees with ChatGPT less than you'd think: for a typical question, only 2 brands were named by Gemini, ChatGPT and AI Mode alike ([brand overlap](/reports/chatgpt-gemini-ai-mode-brand-overlap-2026)).

The longer list is good news for smaller brands. A fifth or sixth slot is a real opening. The weak spot is ratings: Gemini got the rating wrong most often where a review app had added a second copy of the product data, right just 2 times out of 8.

## What to do with this

1. **Name your certifications plainly** on product pages, with the certifying body.
2. **Use one Product block** with your rating in it, so Gemini doesn't pick the wrong number.
3. **Test Gemini separately.** Its list differs from ChatGPT's.
4. **Make your price easy to find** on the product page and in structured data, since Gemini rarely volunteers it.

Testing where Gemini names you is part of [Close the Gap](/close-the-gap). The [AI Visibility Check](/ai-visibility-check) is a quick first look.

## How this was done

- **Questions:** 48 shopping questions across supplements, beauty, food and drink, pet, home and apparel, asked in Gemini, ChatGPT and Google AI Mode in fresh sessions, November 4 to 11, 2026.
- **Fact checks:** each AI's stated price and rating compared with 24 product pages.
- **Limits:** one run per question per engine, and Gemini changes often.

No store or brand is named in this report.
