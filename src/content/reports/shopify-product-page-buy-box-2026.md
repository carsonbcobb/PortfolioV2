**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 17, 2026.**

The buy box is the few inches around the price and the add to cart button. It's where shoppers decide, and where most product page tests I run happen. I checked what 596 Shopify brand product pages put there.

## The short version

- **63% have a quantity selector.**
- **43% show dynamic checkout buttons** like Shop Pay or Buy it now.
- **37% have sticky add to cart code.**
- **34% mention a guarantee or free returns** in the page text.
- **11% show a delivery estimate.** Arrives by, order within, ships today.
- **8% use low stock messages and 5% show installment messaging** in the page source.
- **51% load product recommendations.**

## What the gaps cost

Baymard Institute's research puts the average documented cart abandonment rate at 70.22%, and the top reasons are extra costs (40% of shoppers who abandoned), slow delivery (20%) and not trusting the site (19%) ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate-statistics)). The buy box can answer all three before the cart: the total cost, when it arrives, and why it's safe to try.

Most stores answer one at best. 11% say when it arrives. 34% offer a guarantee in words. Supplements stores lead on guarantees, at 53%, and food stores trail at 15%.

Dynamic checkout buttons are a real tradeoff. They speed up one product purchases, especially on mobile with Shop Pay. They also skip the cart, where bundles, free shipping progress and subscription upsells live. I wouldn't add or remove them without a test.

## What to do with this

1. **Add a delivery estimate.** Even a simple "Order by 2pm, ships today" line.
2. **State the guarantee in one line under the button.** Window and what's covered.
3. **Show the shipping threshold** where the price is, not only in the cart.
4. **Test dynamic checkout buttons** against your average order value, not just conversion rate.
5. **Keep the buy box short on mobile.** Every line above the button pushes it down.

## How this was done

- **Stores:** 599 Shopify brand stores in six product areas: supplements (118), beauty (125), food and drink (152), pet (41), home (72) and apparel (91), from my dataset of 620 Shopify brand stores, client stores excluded. 596 product pages were readable.
- **When:** November 17, 2026.
- **Pages:** the product page of the first available product in each store's public product feed.
- **How:** page source as first sent by the server. Text checks are keyword patterns (for example "arrives by", "order within", "only 3 left", "money back", "free returns"). Sticky add to cart and dynamic checkout were detected from code patterns.
- **Limits:** apps that add widgets with JavaScript after load, like many subscription, installment and delivery date apps, aren't fully counted, so real shares for those are higher.

No store or brand is named in this report.
