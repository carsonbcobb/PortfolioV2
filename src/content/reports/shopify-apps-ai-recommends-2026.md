**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 24, 2026.**


## Key findings

- **Same top pick in only 5 of 11 questions.** The AIs agreed on Judge.me for reviews, the official ChatGPT app for AI visibility, Smile.io for loyalty, Intelligems for A/B testing and AfterSell for post purchase upsells. They disagreed on email and SMS (Omnisend vs Klaviyo), bundles, search and filter, upsells, back in stock alerts and FAQ apps.
- **The shortlists overlap more than the top picks.** 30 of the 57 apps named were named by both AIs. For FAQ and schema apps, they had no app in common.
- **The App Store listing is the main source.** 17 of 24 answers linked to Shopify App Store listings or categories. ChatGPT quoted App Store ratings and review counts as reasons to pick an app.
- **App makers' own "best apps" articles feed Google AI Mode.** In 7 of 12 Google AI Mode answers, at least one source was a "best apps" article published by an app company, such as Rebuy, AfterSell, Zipify and Convert.
- **Every ChatGPT answer carried an ad.** All 12 showed a sponsored placement under the answer. Joy Loyalty appeared as the ad on 3 questions and wasn't in ChatGPT's own loyalty answer. Ordergroove advertised on the subscriptions question.
- **The native Shopify option comes first more often than you'd think.** Google AI Mode recommended Shopify's own Search & Discovery app first for search and filter, and offered Shopify Subscriptions, Shopify Bundles and Shopify Messaging as starting points.

## Where does AI get its app recommendations?

**The Shopify App Store.** Listings and category pages were linked in 17 of 24 answers. ChatGPT often repeated the rating and review count from the listing, for example "5.0/5, 6,842 reviews" for Kaching Bundles, and used them to rank its picks.

**App makers' comparison articles.** Google AI Mode often cited "best apps" posts written by app companies, which naturally feature their own product. On the upsell questions, it cited articles from Rebuy, AfterSell and Zipify.

**Reddit and the Shopify Community.** Google AI Mode cited r/shopify threads on reviews, bundles, loyalty and A/B testing.

**App websites.** ChatGPT linked straight to app makers' own sites more often, for example judge.me, smile.io, intelligems.io and klaviyo.com.

## What this means if you're choosing an app

Use AI to build a shortlist, not to make the decision. The two AIs agreed on the top pick less than half the time, and both lean on ratings and articles that app makers influence. Check three things yourself: whether the app adds code or data that conflicts with what's already on your store (review apps and FAQ apps often add their own structured data), what it costs at your order volume, and whether it works with your subscription, email and reviews stack.

On structured data specifically: in my research, [29% of the Shopify stores I checked publish duplicate product data](/reports/duplicate-product-schema-ai-visibility), usually from a review app. If you add an FAQ or schema app, check that it doesn't add a second Product block.

## What this means if you make a Shopify app

1. **Your App Store listing is your AI landing page.** It was the most linked source. Keep the description specific about who the app is for and what it does, and keep reviews coming.
2. **Ratings travel.** ChatGPT quoted rating and review counts by number. A listing with few reviews loses to one with thousands.
3. **Comparison articles get cited.** Google AI Mode used app makers' "best apps" posts as sources. A fair comparison that answers the merchant's question gets used.
4. **Show up for the native option.** When Shopify has its own app for the job, AI often recommends it first. Explain clearly when a merchant has outgrown it.

## How we ran this test

- **Questions:** 12 questions written the way Shopify merchants ask about apps (for example: "Which search and filter app is best for a Shopify store with a few hundred products?"). Full list in [shopify_apps_ai_answers.csv](/data/reports/shopify-apps-ai-recommends-2026/shopify_apps_ai_answers.csv).
- **AIs:** Google AI Mode (US and English settings, signed in to a Google account) and ChatGPT (logged out, default model). Each question asked once per AI. 24 answers in total.
- **Date:** November 24, 2026.
- **What we recorded:** the top pick (when the AI named one), every app named, the links and sources shown, and any sponsored placement.
- **Matching:** apps were matched by name across the two AIs. Shopify's own apps (Search & Discovery, Subscriptions, Bundles, Messaging) count as apps.
- **Limits:** One snapshot, one question per topic. App recommendations change with wording, store size and budget, and both AIs asked follow up questions to narrow their picks.
