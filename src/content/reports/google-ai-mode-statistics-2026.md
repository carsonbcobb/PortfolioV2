**By Carson Cobb. Updated November 17, 2026.**

Google AI Mode is the conversational search tab Google opened to everyone in the US in 2025 and expanded through 2026. These are the numbers worth knowing, each with its source, plus my own measurements of what AI Mode recommends when people shop.

| Statistic | Figure | Source and date |

## What it means for brands

AI Mode questions are longer, more specific and more often follow ups, which is how shoppers narrow a decision. "Which" questions grew 40%. A product page that only targets "best protein powder" misses "which protein powder is easiest on my stomach if I'm lactose intolerant", and that's the kind of question AI Mode is getting.

Because AI Mode builds on Google's index, the classic basics still count: indexable pages, structured data, a Merchant Center feed. But ranking isn't the same as being recommended, and the brands AI Mode names depend heavily on what other sites say about them.
