**By Carson Cobb. Research from November 4 to 17, 2026.**

ChatGPT has 900 million weekly users, according to OpenAI ([TechCrunch, February 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users)). Google AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users, according to Google ([Search Engine Journal, May 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-shares-first-ai-mode-usage-data/)). Most brands treat them as one channel. My tests say they shop differently.

## Side by side

| Measure | ChatGPT | Google AI Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brands per shopping answer, median | 4 | 5 |
| Answers showing prices | 38 of 48 (79%) | 30 of 48 (63%) |
| Answers asking about the shopper or offering to narrow | 18 of 48 | 6 of 48 |
| Answers with "best for" labels | 44 of 48 | 29 of 48 |
| Labels naming a specific need | 45% | 51% |
| Answers citing certifications | 12 | 25 |
| Reddit as a visible source | 1 of 48 answers | 4 of 19 answers in a separate test |
| Correct price for a specific product | 22 of 24 | 19 of 24 |
| Correct rating for a specific product | 17 of 23 | 12 of 22 |

In the separate 19 question test, 11 AI Mode answers were built around product listings with a price, rating, review count and seller.

## The difference in one line each

**ChatGPT acts like a knowledgeable friend.** It asks about you, leans on review and testing sites, gives a short list with reasons, and links out.

**AI Mode acts like a smarter Google Shopping.** It builds answers around product listings from Google's own data, quotes certifications and claims, and favors brand stores more than Google's first page does: 37% of the sites it cited were brand stores, against 16% of first page results.

## What it means for a brand

| To win in | Focus on |
| --- | --- |
| Both | Specific, checkable claims; who each product is for; one clean Product block |
| ChatGPT | Coverage on review and testing sites; answers to the follow up questions it asks |
| AI Mode | A complete Merchant Center feed with GTINs, variants, shipping and returns; certifications stated plainly |

Test both. For a typical question, only about 2 brands were named by ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Mode alike. There's also a separate comparison of [how Gemini recommends products](/reports/how-gemini-chooses-products-2026).

Testing where each engine names you is part of [Close the Gap](/close-the-gap). The [AI Visibility Check](/ai-visibility-check) is a quick first look.

## How this was done

- **Main test:** 48 shopping questions across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, November 4 to 11, 2026.
- **Separate AI Mode test:** 19 shopping questions compared in Google search and AI Mode on November 14, 2026.
- **Fact checks:** stated prices and ratings compared with 24 product pages.
- **Limits:** one run per question per engine. Both products change often.

No store or brand is named in this report.
