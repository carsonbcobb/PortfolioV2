**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 23 and 24, 2026.**

Disclosure: I'm an independent AI search and CRO specialist for Shopify brands, so I'm part of this market. I was named in 3 of the 28 answers. Every answer is in the data files below, including the ones that named other people.


## Key findings

- **60 providers, little agreement.** Across 28 answers, ChatGPT and Google AI Mode named 60 agencies and consultants. Only 10 were named by both.
- **WRKNG Digital was named most:** 10 of 28 answers, 5 in each AI. iPullRank followed with 8.
- **The two AIs favor different firms.** Siege Media was named in 7 ChatGPT answers and no Google AI Mode answers. Succession Media was named in 6 Google AI Mode answers and 1 ChatGPT answer. Go Fish Digital appeared only in ChatGPT.
- **Google AI Mode cites agencies' own "best agency" lists.** In 5 of 8 Google AI Mode answers where we recorded every source, AI Mode named an agency and cited that same agency's "best agencies" article as the source.
- **ChatGPT leans on reputation signals.** It favored larger SEO agencies and quoted Clutch ratings and review counts as reasons to shortlist them. On one question it showed a map of Los Angeles firms with star ratings.
- **Both AIs said to start with an independent specialist.** Asked whether a Shopify store should hire a GEO agency or a freelancer, both recommended a specialist freelancer or consultant for most stores, and an agency for larger brands that need content and PR at scale.
- **Independent people are rarely named by name.** Several answers described an independent Shopify consultant who pairs AI search with CRO as the right fit, then named only agencies. Only a handful of individuals were named.
- **AI search firms are buying ads in ChatGPT.** In 4 of the 6 ChatGPT answers where we checked for ads, a sponsored placement came from an AI visibility tool or an SEO agency.

## Where does AI get its agency recommendations?

**Google AI Mode** built most of its agency answers from articles titled like "best GEO agencies for Shopify." Many of these are published by agencies that appear in their own lists, and AI Mode cited them as its sources. It also cited the Shopify Community, Reddit threads in r/aeo, r/b2bmarketing and r/DigitalMarketing, and agencies' own service pages.

**ChatGPT** cited agencies' own websites more than lists, and favored firms with a long track record and public reviews. It mentioned Clutch ratings by number, for example 4.9 from 48 reviews and 4.8 from 167 reviews, and added cautions like "a fit-based shortlist, not a definitive independently verified ranking."

What this means for anyone choosing a provider: an AI answer about agencies is mostly a summary of what agencies say about themselves, plus review platforms. Use it to build a shortlist, then check the work.

## Should a Shopify brand hire an agency or an independent specialist?

Both AIs gave the same answer. For most Shopify stores, start with an independent specialist who does the technical work directly. Choose an agency when you need ongoing content production and digital PR at scale, or you run a large, complex catalog.

The reasons they gave:

| | Independent specialist | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Who does the work | The person you hire | A team behind an account manager |
| Best for | Schema, product data, feeds, crawler access, product page fixes | Ongoing content, digital PR and large catalogs |
| Cost | Lower | Higher, often with longer commitments |
| Risk | Limited capacity | Slower handoffs between team members |

Google AI Mode put it this way for skincare brands: look for "an independent Shopify consultant who pairs AI search optimization with Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) so that the traffic you gain actually converts."

| Freelance specialist | $500 to $3,000 per project | Not separated out |
| Monthly retainer, small or boutique | $1,500 to $5,000 a month | $1,500 to $5,000 a month |
| Full service agency | $5,000 to $15,000+ a month | $5,000 to $10,000+ a month |

ChatGPT also cited published ecommerce agency pricing of about $2,000 to $6,000 a month.

For reference, my own pricing is published: Close the Gap Focus is $2,500 a month and Close the Gap Full is $5,000 a month.

## How to choose, based on what both AIs told buyers to check

1. **Ask what they'll change on your store.** Schema, product data, feeds, crawler access and product page copy should be specific, not "content strategy."
2. **Ask to see AI answers before and after.** In ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, for questions your customers actually ask.
3. **Check who does the work.** The person you talk to, or a team you'll never meet.
4. **Ask how AI traffic will turn into orders.** Visibility without conversion work leaves money on the page.
5. **Be wary of guarantees.** ChatGPT warned that nobody can guarantee it will recommend a brand.

## Where I fit, and where I don't

I'm a good fit if you're a Shopify brand that wants one specialist to track what ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode say about your products, fix the store side (product data, structured data, conflicting facts, feeds, product pages), and make the pages AI sends shoppers to convert. The person who audits your store is the person who fixes it.

I'm not the right fit if you need large scale content production, a digital PR team, or paid media management. An agency built for those will serve you better.

## How we ran this test

- **Questions:** 14 questions written the way Shopify founders and marketers ask when looking for help (for example: "My DTC brand gets traffic from Google but almost none from ChatGPT. Who specializes in fixing that for Shopify stores?"). Full list in [ai_search_help_answers.csv](/data/reports/who-ai-recommends-ai-search-agencies-shopify-2026/ai_search_help_answers.csv).
- **AIs:** Google AI Mode (US and English settings, signed in to a Google account) and ChatGPT (logged out, default model). Each question asked once per AI. 28 answers in total.
- **Dates:** November 23 and 24, 2026.
- **What we recorded:** every agency, consultant or person named as a provider, the sources and links shown, and any sponsored placement. Software tools and marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Shopify apps) were not counted as providers.
- **Sources:** For 8 of the Google AI Mode answers we recorded every source link shown; for the others we recorded the visible sources.
- **Limits:** One snapshot. AI answers change over time and from person to person, and agency answers are especially sensitive to wording. I'm a provider in this market; I've reported the answers as given, including those that didn't name me.
