**By Carson Cobb. Data collected November 4 to 17, 2026.**

I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode eight beauty shopping questions, including a Korean moisturizer for sensitive skin, clean skincare brands, a lip stain that lasts all day and foundation for dark skin tones, and recorded every brand named. Then I scanned 127 beauty brand stores on Shopify and compared them with 617 Shopify brand stores overall.

## The short version

- **4 of 127 beauty stores in my dataset were named at least once.** 3%, against 11% across all six product areas.
- **ChatGPT named a median of 3 brands per answer.** Gemini 4, Google AI Mode 4.5.
- **The engines agree on few brands.** A median of 1.5 per question were named by all three.
- **51 different brands** were named across the eight questions.

## How beauty stores compare

| Measure | Beauty stores | All 617 stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization structured data on the homepage | 71% | 75% |
| Product structured data | 86% | 86% |
| GTIN in product structured data | 15% | 23% |
| Variant data with ProductGroup | 6% | 23% |
| Return or shipping terms in structured data | 10% | 11% |

| No structured data on the homepage | 25% | 19% |

## What stands out

Beauty was the hardest product area to get named in. Only 4 of the 127 beauty stores in my dataset came up in any answer, 3%, against 11% overall. AI named a median of just 3 brands per beauty answer in ChatGPT, and the engines agreed on fewer brands than in any other area.

Beauty questions are personal: skin type, tone, hair type, sensitivity. In my research, ChatGPT often asked a follow up about skin type before recommending anything. Beauty product pages are good at how to use (51%) but weaker at saying who a product is for (34%), and only 6% describe shades or sizes as variants in structured data. Beauty also had the fewest GTINs of any area, 15%, which matters because the same products are often sold through several retailers.

## What to do with this

1. **Say who it is for, in the shopper's words.** Skin type, tone range, hair type, sensitivity.
2. **Describe shades as variants.** Each shade with its own name, GTIN and stock.
3. **Match your retailer listings.** Same product names, GTINs and claims at every retailer.
4. **Answer the follow up question on the page.** If AI would ask "what's your skin type", your page should already cover each answer.

## How this was done

- **AI answers:** eight beauty shopping questions, part of 48 across six product areas, asked in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode between November 4 and 11, 2026. Every brand named was recorded.
- **Stores:** 127 beauty stores within 617 Shopify brand stores, my full dataset of 620 minus client stores and one store that blocked automated reads, scanned November 16 and 17, 2026. Product page checks used the first product in each store's public feed, read as the server first sends it.
- **Limits:** eight questions can't cover every product a store sells, so an unnamed brand may simply not fit the questions asked. Comparisons show correlation, not cause.

No store or brand is named in this report.
